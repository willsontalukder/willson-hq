# The Perception Effect · text-to-video prompts (complete)

Each prompt stands on its own: paste it straight into Google Flow (text to video). One prompt = one ~8-second clip.
Add the voice-over, the scores (4.5 / 9.5), titles and the infographic in Premiere Pro.

## 01A · Hook: street plate
```
Fast handheld close-up at a small Dhaka street food stall at night. A portion of chicken biryani with saffron-yellow rice, one piece of chicken, a boiled egg and a potato is dropped onto a thin white paper plate on a dented steel table. A plastic spoon is stuck in the rice. One bare white tube light overhead, steam rising from a huge biryani pot behind. Sound: plate tap, busy traffic, rickshaw bells. Cinematic documentary style, realistic, full-frame cinema camera, shallow depth of field, 16:9, no on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos.
```
