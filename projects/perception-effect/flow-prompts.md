# The Perception Effect · Google Flow (Veo) prompts

Same dish, two settings: a Dhaka street stall vs. a fine-dining room. Each prompt makes one ~8-second clip.
Total: 16 clips (~2 min of picture). Add Frank's own voice-over, the scores, titles and the infographic in Premiere Pro to reach 3–4 min.

## How to use these in Flow

1. **Make the characters first.** In Flow, generate one reference image of each character (HOST, P1, P2, CHEF) with the descriptions below, then add them as "ingredients" to every clip they appear in. This keeps faces the same from shot to shot.
2. **Paste the whole prompt**, including the STYLE line at the end, every time. Veo has no memory between clips.
3. **No on-screen text from Veo.** Add scores (4.5 / 9.5), names and graphics in Premiere; AI text is often misspelled.
4. **Dialogue:** keep each line under about 8 seconds. Veo's English speech is more reliable than Bangla. For Bangla, record the lines yourself, or dub them, and generate clips without dialogue.
5. Generate 2–4 takes per clip and pick the most natural one.

## Honesty note (important)

Every clip here is AI-generated, so the "participants" and their ratings are not real data. Label the video clearly, e.g. **"AI dramatization of a real psychology effect"**, and in the description cite real research: the 2008 Plassmann et al. wine study (people rated the same wine higher when told it cost more). Don't present the 4.5 vs 9.5 scores as results of a real experiment. A stronger version: run the real test with 6–10 friends and use AI only for the hook and B-roll.

## Reusable blocks (copy exactly)

**STYLE:** `Cinematic documentary style, realistic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera, natural skin tones, shallow depth of field, 16:9, no on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark.`

**HOST:** `a Bangladeshi man in his late 20s, short wavy black hair, thin short beard, plain black t-shirt and dark jeans, friendly and confident presenter`
**P1:** `a Bangladeshi woman in her early 30s, shoulder-length black hair, mustard-yellow kurti, small gold earrings`
**P2:** `a Bangladeshi man in his mid 40s, grey-streaked short hair, round metal glasses, light-blue checked shirt`
**CHEF:** `a Bangladeshi chef in his 30s, neatly trimmed beard, white chef jacket, black apron`
**DISH:** `a portion of chicken biryani with saffron-yellow rice, one piece of chicken, a boiled egg and a potato`
**SET A (street):** `a small Dhaka street food stall at night, tin roof, one bare white tube light, steel table, red plastic stools, a huge steaming biryani pot, busy street with rickshaw bells and traffic noise`
**SET B (luxury):** `an upscale fine-dining restaurant, warm dim amber lighting, dark wood table with white tablecloth, candles, crystal glasses, soft jazz piano in the background`

## Scene 01 · Hook (fast cuts, upbeat)

**01A · Street plate**
```
Fast handheld close-up: DISH is dropped onto a thin white paper plate on a dented steel table at SET A. A plastic spoon is stuck in the rice. Harsh white tube light, steam rising. Sound: plate tap, street traffic, rickshaw bell. STYLE
```

**01B · Luxury plate**
```
Slow cinematic push-in on the same DISH beautifully plated on a matte black ceramic plate at SET B: rice shaped in a neat ring, egg halved, microgreens and a drizzle of saffron sauce, candlelight glinting off silver cutlery. Sound: soft jazz piano, a gentle clink of glass. STYLE
```
*(In Premiere: show 01A and 01B side by side as a split screen under your voice-over: "Can the exact same meal score a 4 in one room and a 10 in another?")*

## Scene 02 · Setup

**02A · Host between the two sets**
```
Medium-wide shot inside a large studio where two sets are built side by side: on the left SET A street stall, on the right SET B fine-dining table. HOST stands in the middle, looks into the camera and says with energy: "Today, we test how environment, plating and names change what we taste." Camera slowly pushes in. STYLE
```

**02B · Host reveal of the chef**
```
Medium shot: HOST gestures toward CHEF, who is preparing two portions of DISH side by side on a stainless steel kitchen counter. CHEF nods and smiles at the camera. Bright, clean kitchen light. Sound: sizzling pan, kitchen ambience. STYLE
```

## Scene 03 · Control (street, candid, raw)

**03A · P1 is served**
```
Handheld documentary shot at SET A: P1 sits on a red plastic stool. A street vendor slides DISH on a paper plate in front of her with a plastic spoon. She looks at it a little unsure. Sound: street noise, vendor says "Here you go." STYLE
```

**03B · P1 tastes and rates**
```
Close-up of P1 at SET A under harsh white tube light. She takes a bite, chews, shrugs slightly and says casually: "It's decent... maybe a four or five out of ten. Nothing special." Raw audio, slightly shaky handheld camera. STYLE
```

**03C · P2 tastes and rates**
```
Medium close-up of P2 at SET A eating DISH from a paper plate with a plastic spoon. He tilts his head and says: "Honestly, it's okay. Normal biryani. Four and a half." Street traffic behind him. STYLE
```

## Scene 04 · Variable (luxury, cinematic, soft music)

**04A · Plating in macro**
```
Extreme macro shot in a fine-dining kitchen: CHEF uses tweezers to place microgreens on DISH on a black ceramic plate, then drizzles saffron sauce in a thin line. Warm side light, very shallow depth of field. Sound: quiet kitchen, soft music. STYLE
```

**04B · Waiter presents the dish**
```
Slow dolly shot at SET B: a waiter in a black vest places the plated DISH in front of P1 and announces elegantly: "Chef's signature Saffron Heritage Kacchi." P1 sits up and smiles, impressed. Candlelight, soft jazz. STYLE
```

**04C · P1 tastes and rates**
```
Close-up of P1 at SET B in warm amber candlelight. She takes a small bite with a silver fork, closes her eyes, and says: "Wow... the flavours are so layered. Easily a nine and a half. You can taste the quality." Soft jazz. STYLE
```

**04D · P2 tastes and rates**
```
Medium close-up of P2 at SET B, napkin on his lap, tasting DISH with a silver fork. He nods slowly, impressed, and says: "This is restaurant level. Nine, maybe ten." Warm light, shallow depth of field, candles in the background. STYLE
```

## Scene 05 · Reveal (genuine shock)

**05A · Host reveals the truth**
```
Medium shot at SET B: HOST walks in holding a tablet and shows P1 and P2 the screen. He says calmly: "Both meals were made by the same chef, with the same ingredients." Camera holds on him. Warm light. STYLE
```

**05B · Reactions**
```
Two-shot of P1 and P2 at SET B reacting in genuine surprise: P1 covers her mouth and laughs, P2 takes off his glasses and shakes his head, smiling in disbelief. Handheld, quick slight push-in. Sound: surprised laughter, soft jazz. STYLE
```

**05C · Same plates side by side**
```
Top-down shot: on a plain wooden table, the paper-plate DISH on the left and the black-plate fine-dining DISH on the right. CHEF's hands point to both and then place a spoon of the same rice from one pot onto each plate. Neutral daylight. STYLE
```
