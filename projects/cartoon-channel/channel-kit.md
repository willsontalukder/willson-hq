# Oops! Toons · YouTube channel kit (A to Z)

## 1 · Name
**Channel: Oops! Toons** · Show: **Mishti & Moto**
Why: "Oops!" is a word people say every day, in every country and almost every language. It's exactly the moment every slapstick episode is built on, so the name, the content and the reaction match. Short, easy to say, easy to remember.

**Handle (pick the first that's free):** `@oopstoons` → `@oopstoonstv` → `@oopsmishtimoto` → `@oopstoons.official`
Use the same handle on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.

Backup names, also everyday words: **Uh-Oh Toons**, **Yay Toons**, **Hehe Toons**.

## 4 · Channel settings (YouTube Studio → Settings)
- **Channel → Basic info:** country **Bangladesh**; keywords (below)
- **Channel → Advanced settings → Audience:** choose **"No, set this channel as not made for kids"**, then set each video yourself. If an episode is clearly aimed at young children, mark that video "made for kids". It's a legal requirement, so answer honestly.
- **Upload defaults:** category **Comedy**, language **None / No spoken language** (or English for the titles), license Standard YouTube, comments **on** (hold potentially inappropriate comments for review).
- **Upload defaults → Altered or synthetic content:** answer honestly. Cartoon animation that clearly isn't realistic usually doesn't need the label; realistic-looking AI scenes do.
- **Branding:** profile picture, banner, and a **video watermark** (the logo, shown at the end of videos).
- **Customisation → Layout:** channel trailer for non-subscribers = your best 60 s episode; featured sections: Popular Shorts, Latest Shorts, playlists.
- **Links:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook page.

## 6 · SEO for every video

**Shorts title formula:** `[Character] + [problem] + Oops! + 1 emoji`, under 60 characters.
Examples:
- `Moto vs the Falling Ice Cream 🍦 Oops!`
- `Mishti's Tea Cup Tower… Oops! ☕`
- `When the Umbrella Won't Open 🌧️ #oops`

**Long video title:** `Mishti & Moto | 10 Funniest Oops! Moments 😂 (Cartoon Compilation)`

**Description template:**
```
[One funny sentence about this episode.] 😄

Mishti, a tiny clever kitten, and Moto, a big clumsy dog, turn everyday moments into silent slapstick comedy.

▶ More episodes: [playlist link]
🔔 Subscribe for a new Oops! every day: [channel link]

#cartoon #funnycartoon #mishtiandmoto #oopstoons #catanddog #shorts
```
- **First 2 lines matter most:** they appear in search and under the video.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 only. The first three show above the title.
- **Tags (in the Tags field):** `mishti and moto, oops toons, funny cartoon, cat and dog cartoon, cute kitten cartoon, clumsy dog, slapstick comedy, silent cartoon, [episode topic]`
- **Long videos:** add **chapters** in the description (`0:00 The Ice Cream`, `1:05 The Kite`, …).

**On every video:**
- **Shorts:** link the related long video (the "Related video" option when uploading) so Shorts viewers move to watch hours.
- **Long videos:** end screen with "Subscribe" + "Best for viewer"; add it to a playlist.
- **Thumbnail (long videos):** one big expressive face (Moto shocked or laughing), the object of the gag, bright background, 1–2 words max ("OOPS!").
- **Captions:** none needed (no dialogue), but add sound-effect text on screen if it adds to the joke.

## 7 · Growth habits
- **Post time:** about 8:00 AM Dhaka (US evening, Europe morning). Same time every day.
- **First hour:** pin a comment asking a question ("What should Moto break next? 👇") and reply to every comment.
- **Community tab** (unlocks as the channel grows): polls like "Who's funnier, Mishti or Moto?"
- **Cross-post** the same Shorts to Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook Reels, without other apps' watermarks.
- **Every Sunday:** look at "viewed vs swiped away" and average view duration. Make more of what works.
