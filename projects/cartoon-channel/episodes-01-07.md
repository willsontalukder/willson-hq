## How to use this file

- **Every shot prompt below is complete and standalone.** Copy the whole grey box into Google Flow. The style, location and character text inside it is copied word for word from the LOCK blocks in `character-bible.md`; do not edit those sentences. Only the `Shot`, `Action` and `Sound effects` lines change from shot to shot.
- **Attach the reference images** from the bible (Mishti sheet, Moto sheet, the location plate) as ingredients for every shot. Set the aspect ratio to 9:16 in Flow as well.
- **Each clip is ~8 s; the edit uses part of it.** The `Edit` time is where the shot sits in the finished ~45 s Short. Trim hard: slapstick lives on cutting a frame early.
- **Loops:** the last shot of every episode ends on the same framing and action as the first shot. In Premiere, cut the end on the frame that matches the first frame of shot 1, so the Short replays seamlessly. If Flow offers a start/end frame mode, you can give the last shot the first frame of shot 1 as its end frame.
- **Sound:** Veo adds its own sound effects. Treat them as a guide track. For the final mix, rebuild the key hits (boing, honk, splash, the 'ting!') in Premiere from a licensed SFX library so every episode has the same signature sounds. Strip any accidental voice or music.
- **Formula in every episode:** Hook (0–2 s, the problem in one image) → Try (Moto's clumsy fixes make it worse) → Twist (Mishti's Slow Blink, Idea Ping, clever fix) → Payoff + loop back to the first frame.

## Episode 01 · Moto vs the Falling Ice Cream 🍦 Oops!

- **Title** (37 characters): `Moto vs the Falling Ice Cream 🍦 Oops!`
- **Logline:** Moto's two-scoop ice cream starts to topple, and every clumsy rescue sends the top scoop higher, until Mishti catches it in a tea cup.
- **The one feeling:** A friend who catches what you drop is worth more than the ice cream.
- **Location:** Tea stall lane · **Runtime:** about 45 s · **From idea:** 1. The falling ice cream
- **Props (keep identical in every shot):** Ice cream: a crunchy golden waffle cone with one mango-yellow scoop and one pistachio-green scoop on top. Same cone in every shot.

## Episode 02 · Moto's First Kite Won't Fly 🪁 Oops!

- **Title** (35 characters): `Moto's First Kite Won't Fly 🪁 Oops!`
- **Logline:** Moto runs and jumps and spins, but his kite only drags behind him, until Mishti shows him which way the wind blows.
- **The one feeling:** Sometimes you just need a friend to turn you around.
- **Location:** Rooftop · **Runtime:** about 45 s · **From idea:** 5. Moto's first kite
- **Props (keep identical in every shot):** Kite: a small diamond-shaped paper kite in plain kite red with a yellow tail of paper bows, white string on a wooden reel (latai). Same kite in every shot.

### Storyboard

| Shot | Edit | Beat | What we see |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | 0–3 s | HOOK | Moto runs proudly across the middle of the rooftop, grinning, holding a wooden string reel high in one front paw. Behind him his little red paper kite does not fly at all: it drags along the concrete floor, scraping and bouncing, its yellow tail of paper bows flopping. |
| 2.2 | 3–9 s | TRY 1 | The dragging kite bonks gently into the side of a blue water tank. Moto stops and looks back with a confused Head Tilt, so far that his up-ear touches his shoulder. |
| 2.3 | 9–15 s | TRY 2 (worse) | Blinded by the kite on his head, Moto spins around and around trying to shake it off. The kite string wraps around and around his body until he is tied up like a round parcel, with only his nose, his up-ear, his floppy ear and his wagging tail sticking out. |
| 2.4 | 15–22 s | TWIST | Mishti sits on the water-tank stand. She gives one very slow blink and presses a paw to her face. |
| 2.5 | 22–29 s | TWIST (unwrap) | Mishti trots over to the wrapped-up Moto, takes the loose end of the kite string in her teeth and trots away with it. | 2.6 | 29–37 s | TWIST (it flies) | Mishti gently pushes Moto's leg to turn him around to face the other way, into the wind, and points her paw. Moto lifts the little red kite in the breeze. |
| 2.7 | 37–45 s | PAYOFF + LOOP | Moto does his Full-Body Wiggle of joy, then turns and runs happily towards Mishti to hug her, running the wrong way, away from the wind. The string goes slack and the red kite flops down out of the sky and drags behind him again, scraping and bouncing along the floor, while he runs grinning with the reel held high. |
