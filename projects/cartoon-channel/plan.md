# Cartoon Shorts channel · plan (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027)

## Decision
**Series: "Mishti & Moto"**: a tiny, clever orange kitten (Mishti) and a big, clumsy, kind-hearted dog (Moto) in a colourful city inspired by Dhaka: rickshaws, tea stalls, rooftops, rain.
**Style:** silent slapstick comedy (no dialogue, like classic silent cartoons), so it works in every country. 30–60 s Shorts, 3D cartoon look.
**Audience:** families and everyone (not made only for small kids). If a video is mainly for young children you must mark it "Made for kids"; general-audience comedy keeps comments, notifications and better ad rates.
Backup series ideas: "Grandpa Rickshaw" (a heart-warming old rickshaw puller and his magic rickshaw), "Little Chef Pip" (a tiny chef's kitchen disasters).
Channel name: **"Mishti & Moto"** (check @mishtiandmoto is free). Backup: "Tiny Tea Stall Toons".

## Why this can be monetised
YouTube does not pay for mass-produced, repetitive "AI slop", or for off-putting or distressing AI videos (policy clarified July 2026). A real series is the opposite:
- the **same original characters** in every episode, each with a personality
- **each episode is a written story** with a setup, a gag and a payoff (not a template)
- **your own editing, timing and sound design**: slapstick lives on timing and sound effects
- no copied characters (no Tom & Jerry, no Bluey look-alikes), no weird or creepy "brainrot" content

## Episode formula (30–60 s)
1. **Hook (0–2 s):** the problem in one image (Moto's ice cream is about to fall).
2. **Try (2–20 s):** Moto's clumsy fix makes it worse.
3. **Twist (20–40 s):** Mishti's clever idea.
4. **Payoff + loop (last 5 s):** a funny ending that leads back into the first frame.

## Production pipeline (one episode ≈ 3–4 h once practised)
1. **Character sheet (once):** front, side and back views of Mishti and Moto, plus 3 locations. Reuse these as reference images every time.
2. **Storyboard:** 6–8 panels (John writes, you approve).
3. **Images:** generate each panel with the character references (Flow "ingredients" or any tool with character reference).
4. **Animation:** image-to-video in Google Flow (Veo) or a similar tool, 4–8 s per shot.
5. **Edit in Premiere Pro:** timing, cartoon sound effects (boing, whoosh, splat), music from the YouTube Audio Library or Suno on a paid plan.
6. **Thumbnail:** a big expressive face plus the problem, with no text or 1–2 words.

Budget: about $30–40 a month (an AI video plan + music). Start with free tiers in week 1.

## Posting
- **5 Shorts a week + one compilation a week** (8–12 minutes of your own episodes plus a new linking scene) for watch hours.
- Post at about **8:00 AM Dhaka time** for US evening and Europe morning.
- Also post on Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook Reels.

## Monetisation path
- YouTube Partner Program: 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days **or** 4,000 watch hours. Compilations make the watch-hour route realistic. Check current numbers in YouTube Studio → Earn.
- Before YPP: Fiverr AI cartoon/animation gigs (use episodes as portfolio), custom cartoon videos for brands and kids' birthdays, and later stickers, merch and licensing if the characters catch on.

## 3-month targets (ranges, not promises)

| | Month 1 (Nov) | Month 2 (Dec) | Month 3 (Jan) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Episodes (Shorts) | 16 | 20 | 22 |

Cartoon channels can grow faster than average when one character becomes recognisable, but YPP is still most likely around month 3–6.
