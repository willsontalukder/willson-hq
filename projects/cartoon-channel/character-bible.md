# Mishti & Moto · Character & World Bible

Show: **Mishti & Moto** · Channel: **Oops! Toons** · Silent 3D slapstick, 9:16 Shorts
Written by John (storyteller), 27 Oct 2026. Original characters; do not change a word of the LOCK blocks without updating every episode file.

**The one idea of the series:** every small disaster ends with the two of them closer than before. Moto breaks it with love, Mishti fixes it with brains, and nobody ever gets hurt.

---

## How to use this bible (read once)

1. **LOCK blocks are law.** Each block in a grey box marked `LOCK` is pasted **word for word** into every Google Flow shot prompt where that character or place appears. Never paraphrase, shorten or "improve" them inside a prompt. If you change a LOCK, change it here first, then regenerate the prompts.
2. **Prompt order is always the same:** `STYLE-HEAD` → location LOCK → character LOCK(s) (only the characters who appear) → the shot (camera, action, sound) → `STYLE-TAIL`.
3. **Reference images first.** Make the sheets in section 5 once, pick the best take of each, and upload them to Flow as ingredients (Mishti sheet, Moto sheet, the location plate) for every shot. Text keeps the look consistent; the reference images do most of the work.
4. **No people on screen, ever.** The city is busy but we never see a person. This keeps every shot consistent and keeps the stars on screen alone.
5. **Set 9:16 (portrait) in Flow's settings as well as in the prompt.** If a model ignores the text, the setting wins.
6. **Left and right:** AI models often mirror shots. Moto's up-ear is his **right** ear; if a take mirrors it, flip the clip horizontally in Premiere (Effect Controls → Transform/Horizontal Flip) unless something in the background would give it away.

## 2 · Mishti

### LOCK (paste word for word)

```text
LOCK · MISHTI
Mishti is a tiny original cartoon kitten: bright tangerine-orange fur with faint darker orange tabby stripes on her back and tail, a cream-white muzzle, chest and paw tips, and a white tip on her tail. Her round head is big for her body, with very large leaf-green eyes with glossy highlights, a small pink nose, short white whiskers and triangular ears with pink insides. Tiny body, short legs, walks on four legs and sits up neatly. She always wears a hand-knitted bright red scarf with two thin white stripes, tied once around her neck with both ends trailing behind. Standing, she is only as tall as Moto's head. Calm, clever, confident face.
```

### Fixed visual spec

| Part | Spec | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Fur | Short, soft, slightly fluffy at cheeks and chest; faint tabby stripes on back, tail and forehead (3 small stripes) | Tangerine orange `#F07A1A`, stripes `#C85A0E` |
| Muzzle, chest, paw tips, tail tip | Cream-white | `#FFF1DC` |
| Eyes | Very large, round, glossy, two white highlights each | Leaf green `#7BC043`, black pupils |
| Nose / ear insides | Small triangle nose; ear insides soft pink | `#F4A3A8` |
| Whiskers | Short, white, 3 per side; they spring up when she has an idea | White |
| Scarf | Hand-knitted rib stitch, two thin white stripes near each end, tied once, both ends trail behind and flutter when she moves | Red `#E8322B`, stripes `#FFFFFF` |

**Proportions:** about 2.5 heads tall when sitting up. Head is roughly 40% of her total body size. Standing on four legs, her ear tips reach the top of Moto's head when she stands beside his head (i.e. she is as tall as Moto's head is high, roughly one fifth of Moto's full height). She fits comfortably sitting on top of Moto's head.

**Movement:** quick, precise, light. She never runs in a panic; she trots, hops and leaps in clean arcs and always lands neatly.

**Personality:** the brains. Calm, clever, a little dry, secretly adores Moto. She never mocks him; she rolls her eyes, then helps. Hates getting wet. Loves tea-stall biscuits and warm windowsills.

**Sounds she makes:** short "mrrp?" (question), soft "mew" (pleased), purr, tiny "hmph" sniff. Never words.

### Signature reactions (use in every episode)

| Name | What it looks like | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| **The Slow Blink** | Watches Moto's disaster, blinks once very slowly, then presses one paw to her face (facepaw) | soft "hmph" |
| **The Idea Ping** | Ears shoot straight up, whiskers spring out, tail curls into a question-mark shape then snaps straight | tiny bell "ting!" |
| **The Ta-Da** | Sits up tall, one front paw raised, chin up, scarf ends flutter as if in a breeze | short "mrrp!" + sparkle chime |
| **The Shake** | When wet: fur puffs into a round fluffy ball, then she shakes it flat | "fluff" whoosh |

### Expression list (for the expression sheet)
1. Calm neutral (half-closed eyes, small smile) 2. Slow blink 3. Facepaw 4. Idea Ping (ears up, eyes wide, whiskers out) 5. Proud Ta-Da (chin up, eyes closed, smile) 6. Surprised (eyes huge, pupils tiny, fur slightly puffed) 7. Disgusted by a lick (tongue out, eyes squeezed) 8. Asleep (curled, contented smile) 9. Laughing (eyes closed, paw over mouth) 10. Soaked and grumpy (fur flat, scarf dripping).

## 3 · Moto

### LOCK (paste word for word)

```text
LOCK · MOTO
Moto is a big, round, chunky original cartoon street dog: short rich chocolate-brown fur with a cream muzzle, chest and belly, a big round shiny black nose, warm amber-brown eyes with glossy highlights and thick dark-brown eyebrows. His right ear stands straight up and his left ear flops down. Pear-shaped body with a wide bottom, short sturdy legs, big round paws, and a long thick tail with a cream tip that wags constantly. He walks on four legs and stands up on his hind legs to hold things in his front paws. He always wears a green-and-yellow checked cotton gamcha towel tied around his neck as a neckerchief, knot at the front. He is about five times taller than Mishti. Goofy, gentle, eager face, often with the tip of his pink tongue showing.
```

### Fixed visual spec

| Part | Spec | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Fur | Short, smooth, slightly shiny | Chocolate brown `#7A4A2A` |
| Muzzle, chest, belly, tail tip | Cream, soft edge where it meets the brown | `#F3DDB8` |
| Nose | Big, round, glossy, about the size of Mishti's head | Black `#1E1410` |
| Eyes | Round, glossy, two highlights, set wide | Amber brown `#B5762E` |
| Eyebrows | Thick fur ridges, very mobile (main acting tool) | Dark brown `#4A2B17` |
| Ears | **Right ear stands up** (pointed, slightly bent at the tip); **left ear flops down** past the cheek | Brown, insides `#E7B7A0` |
| Tongue | Often peeks out at the side of his mouth | Pink `#F28B9A` |
| Gamcha neckerchief | Cotton, large square checks, tied with a knot at the front, two short ends sticking out | Green `#2E9E5B` and yellow `#FFC94A`, thin white lines between checks |

**Proportions:** about 3 heads tall when sitting. Pear-shaped: narrow shoulders, wide round bottom, short legs. About five times Mishti's height. Standing on hind legs, his head is roughly at the height of the tea stall counter's top shelf.

**Movement:** big, heavy, bouncy. Every step has a little wobble; his bottom swings. He overreacts to everything, then recovers instantly. He is strong but gentle: he never knocks anyone over, and when he tumbles he always lands soft and pops straight up, unhurt and smiling.

**Personality:** the heart. Kind, eager, loyal, a little clumsy and very proud of small wins. Tries to fix things himself first, always for someone else's sake (or for food). Thinks Mishti is the cleverest creature alive.

**Sounds he makes:** "woof!" (one, happy), "hrrm?" (confused grunt), whimper-whine (sad but cute), big happy panting, "awoo" (tiny howl of joy), sneeze. Never words.

### Signature reactions (use in every episode)

| Name | What it looks like | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| **The Oops Face** | Both ears (even the floppy one) shoot straight up, eyes go huge, tongue flops out sideways, he freezes for one beat | "boing" + slide whistle down |
| **The Sheepish Grin** | After a mess: closed-mouth wide grin, eyebrows tilted up, rubs the back of his head with one paw | small "heh" grunt |
| **The Helicopter Tail** | Pure joy: tail spins in circles so fast it blurs | propeller "wuh-wuh-wuh" |
| **The Full-Body Wiggle** | Happiest: whole body wiggles from nose to bottom, paws tapping | rubbery "wibble" |
| **The Head Tilt** | Confused: head tilts so far the up-ear touches his shoulder | "hrrm?" |

### Expression list (for the expression sheet)
1. Happy goofy (tongue out) 2. Oops Face 3. Sheepish Grin 4. Head Tilt confused 5. Concentrating (tongue at corner, eyes crossed) 6. Holding breath (cheeks puffed) 7. Big puppy eyes (wobbly, pleading) 8. Overjoyed (eyes closed, huge open smile) 9. Startled jump (fur on end, legs out) 10. Asleep (snoozing, ear over one eye).

### Together
Mishti often rides on Moto's head, sitting between his ears, holding on to the up-ear. That is their signature pose and the thumbnail pose.

## 4 · The three locations

All three are in the same colourful city inspired by old Dhaka. Daylight and bright. Any sign, banner or painting has **no letters at all**: blank boards or flower patterns only.

### Rooftop (kites, water tanks, mango tree)

```text
LOCK · ROOFTOP
A sunny flat rooftop of an old pastel-pink city building inspired by old Dhaka: a smooth pale concrete floor, a low cream parapet wall with a green iron railing around the edge, two big round sky-blue water tanks on a low concrete stand, a clothesline with colourful plain cloths drying, terracotta pots of marigolds, a blue plastic bucket and a small wooden stool beside the tanks, and a leafy mango tree branch hanging over one corner of the roof. Behind, a skyline of colourful old buildings with arched windows, balconies and small domes, and a bright blue afternoon sky full of small diamond-shaped paper kites in plain solid colours. Signboards are blank, with no lettering anywhere.
```

Fixed details: floor `#E9E2D6`, parapet `#FFF4E0`, railing `#2E9E5B`, tanks `#8FD3F4`, building `#F7C5C0`. The action always happens in the middle of the roof, well away from the edge.

### Tea stall lane (rickshaws)

```text
LOCK · TEA STALL LANE
A narrow, cheerful old-Dhaka-inspired lane paved with worn red bricks, lined with pastel buildings in yellow, mint and pink with arched wooden doors and small balconies. A small wooden tea stall with a corrugated tin roof sits on one side: a big dented aluminium kettle steaming on a clay stove, rows of small round terracotta tea cups, glass jars of round biscuits, a little brass bell hanging from the roof, and a long wooden bench painted green in front. Colourful cycle rickshaws are parked along the lane, painted red, yellow and blue with flower and peacock patterns, each with a folded hood, a silver bell and a black rubber bulb horn on the handlebar. Overhead, a tangle of wires with small string lights. Signboards are blank, with no lettering anywhere. No people.
```

Fixed details: bricks `#B5523B`, bench `#2E9E5B`, tea stall wood `#9C6B3F`, tin roof `#A9B4BC`, rickshaw red `#FF5A5F`, rickshaw yellow `#FFC94A`.

### Their small home

```text
LOCK · HOME
Mishti and Moto's small, cosy one-room home in an old-Dhaka-inspired building: whitewashed walls, a turquoise wooden door and a turquoise window with a curly iron grill, a low wooden bed covered with a colourful stitched patchwork quilt, a round red floor cushion where Mishti sleeps, a wooden shelf with shiny steel tiffin boxes and clay pots, a woven straw mat on a polished red cement floor, a small basket of colourful knitting wool, a potted green plant and a slowly turning ceiling fan. ## 6 · Originality and safety rules (check every episode)
```

- **Original designs only.** Not a grey cat and brown mouse, not a blue heeler family, no white-and-black beagle, no Great Dane with spots. Moto's mismatched ears, pear shape and gamcha, and Mishti's knitted red-and-white scarf and green eyes are their trademarks. Never prompt "in the style of" a studio, film or show.
- **Nobody gets hurt.** Falls are soft (cushions, laundry, a bounce), Moto pops up smiling every time, no crying in pain, no injuries, no blood, no fire, no electricity, no heights danger (rooftop action stays in the middle of the roof), no choking, no bad food, no scary faces.
- **No words.** No speech, no singing, no readable text anywhere, including signs, packaging and rickshaw paintings.
- **No people.** The city is lived-in, but people are never on screen.
- **Same props, same colours:** the clay tea cups, the green bench, the red cushion, the blue bucket and the yellow umbrella (rain episodes) look the same every time they appear.
