# Mishti & Moto · why the first test isn't viral, and the fix (28 Oct)

## What the first Flow test (EP01 shot 1.1) got right and wrong
Frank's 8 s test: Moto in the lane holding the two-scoop cone.

**Right:** Moto matches the sheet (ears, gamcha, colours), the lane looks right, 9:16, good 3D quality. The look is solved.

**Wrong:**
- **Nothing happens.** For 8 seconds Moto stands still while the scoop slowly melts. The prompt asked for a topple; Veo gave a melt. No problem, no action, no joke.
- **The camera never moves** and it is a medium shot, not the close-up we asked for.
- **Continuity error at the end:** in the last second the cone floats in the air while Moto's paws open.
- A viewer swipes in under 2 seconds when nothing changes. That's the real reason it doesn't feel viral, not the characters.

## What changes (the new format)
| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| ~45 s, 7 shots | **15–20 s, 4 shots** (Shorts that get replayed beat long ones) |
| Hook "starts to tilt" | **Something already goes wrong in frame 1** (scoop flying, kite crashing) |
| Long prompts with complex action | **One big, simple action per clip**, said first and said strongly ("fast, exaggerated") |
| Text-to-video only | **Make the first frame as an image first** (exact pose, with the character sheet), then animate that frame. Much more control |
| Sound from Veo | **The edit makes the joke:** cut early, freeze-frame on Moto's Oops Face, 2-frame zoom punch-in, loud cartoon SFX, a beat of silence before the payoff |
| Silent, no text | Still no dialogue, but **one short hook caption** in the first 2 s ("wait for it 😂", "he had ONE job 🍦"). Works in every language |

Every episode must pass this test before generating: **Can you describe the gag in one line, and does something funny happen in the first second?**

## Ideas that travel best (use these first)
1. **Food fails** (ice cream, mango, tea, cake): relatable everywhere.
2. **Wholesome turn:** Moto gives his last bite to Mishti. Heart-warming endings get shared, not just watched.
3. **"Wait for it" physics:** a tower, a kite, a stack of cups. Suspense, then a surprise.
4. **Seasonal and trending moments:** rain, Halloween pumpkin, Christmas lights, New Year fireworks, World Cat Day.

## Test plan (no guessing)
- First 2 weeks: post 3 formats (fast food-fail, wholesome, "wait for it") and compare.
- Look at **"viewed vs swiped away"** in YouTube Studio. Aim for **70%+ viewed**. Under 60%: the hook is weak, fix the first second.
- Double down on the format that wins and drop the one that loses.
- No one can promise a viral video. The plan is to test fast and keep what works.
