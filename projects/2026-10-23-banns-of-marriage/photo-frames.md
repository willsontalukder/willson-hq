## 2. Stills settings (A7C, photo mode on the dial)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| File | **RAW + JPEG**. RAW Compressed (smaller files, faster buffer; *check on camera*), JPEG Fine, 3:2, L size |
| Look | Picture Profile **Off**, Creative Style **Standard (-1 / 0 / -1)**, DRO **Off**. The JPEGs match the video and are ready to use; the RAW is only for rescue |
| Exposure mode | **M + Auto ISO**, exposure comp to fine-tune (*check on camera* that comp works in M with Auto ISO) |
| Shutter minimums | **24mm: 1/125** (people moving; 1/250 for the exit and groups) · **85mm: 1/250** (1/200 braced, calm subjects; 1/320–1/500 for hugs and laughter). IBIS helps camera shake, not subject motion |
| Under LED or tube lights (50Hz) | **1/100 or 1/200** where you can, to stay in step with the 100Hz pulse; turn on **Anti-flicker Shoot** if this firmware has it (*check on camera*) |
| Aperture defaults | 85: f/2 for people, f/2.8 for details and two people not side by side · 24: f/2.8 for people, f/5.6–8 for groups and exteriors |
| ISO | Auto ISO 100 to **6400 ceiling**. Up to 12800 only to save a moment (RAW). Prefer noise to blur |
| AF | **AF-C**, Face/Eye AF on (Human, right/left Auto), Focus Area **Tracking: Wide** for people; **Flexible Spot S** for details and rings. AF-S is fine for flat lays |
| Drive | **Continuous Mid** for moments (bursts of 3–5); Single for details and groups. Wait for the card light to stop before switching to video |
| Shutter type | **Mechanical, with Electronic Front Curtain on** (quieter, less shake). **Silent (electronic) shutter only if the church demands silence:** it shows banding under 50Hz LED and tube lights and bends anything moving (rolling shutter). Check the frames after a silent burst. Flash: none owned, never in church |
| White balance | **Same custom WB as the video**, measured by hand at each venue (church face position; again at Green Garden). No AWB. *Check on camera* whether MR recall restores the WB saved in the slot: if it does, save the MR slots with WB set to **Custom (register)** so stills and video read the same measured value |
| Stabilisation | SteadyShot on (stills IBIS on the A7C is automatic; *check on camera*) |

## 3. Hybrid plan: one person, one camera

| Moment | First | Stills plan |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival, exterior, details (09:40–09:55) | **Both:** 10 s clip, then 2–3 stills, per subject | Frames 1–7 |
| Banns reading, readings, **vows, ring exchange** | **VIDEO** (85, mic on, one long take) | **None while rolling.** Frame grabs from the 4K files later (8 MP, good for web and 6×4 prints). Ring hands are recreated after (frame 20) |
| End of rite, blessing | Video | Burst only after the line has ended and the take is stopped |
| Exit | **VIDEO** (24, 100p SM) | Repeat walk for photos (frame 12) |
| Greetings, groups, portraits (10:50–11:20) | **PHOTO** | Video: a 10 s wide of G1 and a few 100p clips of the couple walking |
| Restaurant room, details, food | **Both**, clip first | Frames 21–23 |
| Cake | Video for the cut (SM) | Stills for the feeding moment after (frame 25) |
| **Speeches** | **VIDEO** | Stills only during applause (frame 27) |
| Candids, window portrait, goodbye | **PHOTO** | Short clips only if something lovely is happening |

**Switching rule.** Dial on **MR (1 = 4K 25p, 2 = 100p) = video; dial on M = photo.** Before any scripted moment (readings, vows, rings, cut, speech) the dial is on MR and recording has started **before** the moment begins. Never switch during a sentence someone is saying. The record button also works in photo mode, but then the settings may not be your MR ones (*check on camera*), so don't use it for key moments.
**Grab-a-still tips.** Stop the clip at a natural pause, turn the dial, shoot a 3-frame burst, and turn back. The whole thing takes 5–10 seconds. Keep AF-C and Eye AF in both modes so nothing changes but the dial. Shoot stills from the spot where you filmed, so you don't have to walk. After the burst, wait for the buffer to clear before you need the record button. The A7C can't take a still while it is recording (*check on camera*). If you missed a still, take a frame grab in Premiere instead.

## 4. Cards and batteries with RAW stills

- **Stills:** expect about 400–700 frames. RAW compressed plus JPEG Fine comes to about 30–35 MB a frame, so **about 15–25 GB**.
- **Video:** about 1.5–2 h at 100 Mbps comes to about 70–90 GB. The day total is **about 90–115 GB**. A single 128 GB card is too tight, so plan **two cards of at least 128 GB: card A at the church, card B for lunch** (swap at 11:20).
- **One SD slot:** the A7C can't write stills to a second card at the same time, and there is no in-camera backup. If you have a third card, keep it in your pocket as a spare. A dedicated photo-only card isn't worth the slot swaps on the day. Never swap mid-ceremony.
- **Battery:** Stills add roughly half a battery for 500 shots (Sony's per-charge rating: *check the spec*). Lots of dial switching and reviewing also uses power. Bring **3 batteries minimum** (4 if owned) plus the power bank. Swap the battery at 11:20, not during the vows.
