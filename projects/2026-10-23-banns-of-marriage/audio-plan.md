# Audio Plan · Banns of Marriage · Fri 23 Oct 2026

Owner: James (audio) · Shooter: Frank, solo · Church 10:00 (Farmgate) · Lunch 12:00 (Green Garden, Dhanmondi)

## Gear facts (checked 21 Oct, some still unconfirmed)

- **Kits sold:** 1 TX + 1 RX, or 2 TX + 1 RX + charging case. **Frank: count your transmitters tonight.** This plan works with 1 or 2.
- **Receiver to A7C:** the 3.5mm TRS camera cable goes into the A7C mic jack. Monitor with headphones in the A7C's headphone jack. The Mic Mini 2 receiver has no headphone out.
- **Range:** DJI quotes up to 400 m in open air. Indoors, through walls and bodies, plan for 20–30 m with a clear line of sight.
- **Battery (DJI figures):** TX about 11.5 h, RX about 10.5 h, case about 48 h total. That is plenty for 10:00–14:00.
- **Backup recording NOT confirmed:** sources conflict. DJI's support notes say the Mini 2 transmitter has no storage. The newer Mic Mini 2S records onboard. I could not reach dji.com to confirm. **Assume no transmitter backup.** The phone (below) is the backup.

## Ceremony (church)

**First, ask politely.** Before mic'ing the officiant, ask the priest or church office: "May I clip a tiny wireless mic on you, or place it on the lectern, to record the service for the couple?" If the answer is no, use lectern placement or the phone only. Do not argue.

**Transmitter placement:**

| Moment | 2 transmitters | 1 transmitter |
|---|---|---|
| Vows / Banns | TX1 on the **groom's lapel**, about 15–20 cm below the chin, on the side he turns toward the bride. TX2 on the **officiant** (if allowed) or clipped to the lectern. | **Groom's lapel.** The officiant's lines are picked up through the groom's mic and the phone. |
| Sermon / readings | TX2 on the **lectern** facing the speaker, or on the officiant. Readers change but the lectern stays. | Move the TX to the lectern only at a natural break. Otherwise rely on the phone. |

- **Clipping:** use the magnet so the groom's shirt isn't pinched. Keep it clear of a tie, garland or rustling fabric. Test with him turning his head both ways.
- **Church PA:** if the church uses a PA, the lectern TX still gives cleaner sound than the speakers. A PA feed is not possible without an XLR adapter, so don't ask for one.
- **Phone backup:** put the phone on airplane mode and Do Not Disturb, with the screen locked. Record in the best-quality mode (WAV, or "Lossless" in iPhone Voice Memos). Place it on or near the lectern, mic facing the speaker, on a cloth so knocks don't transfer. Start it at 09:50 and leave it running through the whole service.

## Camera levels and monitoring (A7C)

- Set **Audio Rec Level to manual**. If the receiver has a gain setting, put it in the middle first, then set the level on the camera.
- Aim for **speech peaks around -12 dB** and loud moments no higher than -6 dB. Do a soundcheck with the groom speaking at vow volume.
- Turn **Wind Noise Reduction OFF** indoors. If DJI noise cancellation is available, turn it **OFF**: it can clip consonants, and cleanup is better done in post.
- **Headphones on** for the vows. Check by ear for rustle, dropouts and hum. If you have to run and gun, glance at the meters at least.
- **Room tone:** record 30 s of the empty church in silence (all mics live) before or after the service. Do the same at the restaurant.

## Sync (one person, quick)

1. Start the phone recording, then the camera.
2. Stand in frame, hold the phone near the camera, and **clap once loudly**. Say "Take 1, ceremony."
3. Redo the clap whenever the camera stops or the phone is restarted. If you miss a clap, sync by waveform in post (auto-sync in the NLE works on the matching dialogue).

## Restaurant speeches (12:00, indoor)

- Move the TX to whoever is speaking, or onto the **host's handheld/table position**. With 2 TX, keep one on the groom all day for candid toasts and reactions.
- Background noise (AC, cutlery, music) is the enemy. Ask staff politely to **lower the background music** during speeches. Keep the TX 15–20 cm from the speaker's mouth.
- Put the phone on the head table near the speaker, recording.
- Re-check levels: speech peaks at -12 dB. Laughter and applause will spike, and that's fine.

## Battery plan (10:00–14:00)

- **Night before:** fully charge the TX(s), RX, charging case, phone and every A7C battery. Format the SD card.
- **Power on at 09:40** and keep the RX/TX powered through the service. Between venues, put them in the charging case in the car (11:30–12:00).
- **A7C battery is the weak link** when recording 4K continuously. Carry a spare if Frank owns one; I don't know whether he does. Otherwise use a USB-C power bank, which can top up the camera and phone.
- The phone should be at least 80% with Do Not Disturb on.

## Pre-ceremony audio checklist (09:40)

1. TX(s) clipped and checked (groom; officiant or lectern **with permission**), RX linked and plugged into the A7C mic jack.
2. A7C level set manually, speech peaks around -12 dB, wind filter off, headphones plugged in and hearing the TX.
3. Phone recording (airplane mode, best quality), placed near the lectern on a cloth.
4. Slate clap recorded on camera and phone, then 30 s of room tone.
5. All batteries charged, spare camera battery or power bank in pocket, charging case in the bag.
