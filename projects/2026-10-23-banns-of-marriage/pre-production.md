2. RX → 3.5mm cable → A7C mic jack; headphones in the A7C jack; manual level, peaks -12 dB, max -6 dB.

5. Restaurant: TX to each speaker (or keep TX1 on groom if 2 TX), phone on head table, ask for music down.
