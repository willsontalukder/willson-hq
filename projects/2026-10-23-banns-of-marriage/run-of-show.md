**Weather backup.**

- **If it rains:** both main parts are indoors. Do arrivals and exteriors under the church porch, use a lens cloth and a plastic bag for the kit, and move the couple shots inside. Add 10–15 min to the trip to lunch.
- **If a storm is forecast:** confirm with the couple Thursday night. The ceremony will likely go ahead, but plan on a car rather than a CNG.

**Drone.** Do not fly unless Simon has cleared it. Dhaka near Farmgate and Tejgaon sits close to airport and airbase zones and the Parliament area. Bangladesh's aviation authority (CAAB) permission rules apply. The default is to **leave the DJI Neo 2 at home**.
