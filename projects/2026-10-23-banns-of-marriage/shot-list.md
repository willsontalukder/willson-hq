# Banns of Marriage · Shot List & Camera Plan · Fri 23 Oct 2026

One operator, one A7C, 24mm and 85mm primes, no tripod, gimbal, lights or ND (as far as we know).
Deliverables: 3–5 min highlight film and a full ceremony edit. Items marked *verify* need checking on the camera before Friday.

## 1. Camera settings (same base for both venues, so the footage matches)

| Setting | Church (10:00) | Restaurant (12:00) |
|---|---|---|
| System | **PAL (50Hz)** mode, set the night before. Switching NTSC/PAL needs a card format (*verify*) | same |
| Main video | **4K 25p**, XAVC S 100 Mbps | same |
| Slow motion | **1080p 100p** (normal movie mode, keeps audio), conform to 25p = 4x slow | same |
| Shutter | **1/50** for 25p, **1/100** for 100p. Never 1/60 or 1/125 (flicker) | same |
| Picture profile | **HLG3 gamma, Pro colour, Detail -7** (save to a custom PP slot) | same |
| Exposure | Faces at normal brightness to +1/3. Zebras at 100+ to watch windows | same |
| ISO | Base 125–800. **Ceiling 6400** (prefer 3200 or lower) | same; may need 3200–6400 if dim |
| Aperture | 85mm wide open or 1 stop down; 24mm wide open indoors | same |
| White balance | **Manual Kelvin / custom WB** off a white card on a face position. No AWB | re-set custom WB on arrival |
| Focus | AF-C, Face/Eye AF on, tracking sensitivity 2 (stops jumping to guests), transition speed 5 | same |
| IBIS | SteadyShot on. Use **Active** for 24mm moving shots if this firmware has it (*verify*), Standard for 85mm | same |

**Why 25p, not 30p.** Bangladesh mains is 50Hz, so tungsten, LED and tube lights pulse at 100Hz. At 25p with 1/50 or 1/100 the shutter matches the pulse and the image is clean. 30p at 1/60 bands and flickers under cheap LEDs. 4K 25p also avoids the 1.2x crop the A7C applies at 4K 30p (*verify*). Slow motion therefore goes at 100p and 1/100, not 120p. If an LED still flickers, use Variable Shutter to find the clean speed, or keep that light out of frame.

**Why HLG3 and not S-Log3.** The footage is 8-bit. S-Log3 needs ISO 640 minimum and +1 to +2 stops of overexposure, which a dim church cannot give, so it would come out noisy and band in the grade. HLG3 starts at ISO 125, keeps more highlight room than Standard for the bright windows, and grades to Rec.709 in minutes. If the light is flat and there is no time to grade, PP off (Standard) is an acceptable fallback; do not switch mid-event.

## 3. Handheld without a gimbal or tripod

- Strap around the neck and pulled tight against the body; elbows tucked; viewfinder to the eye (three contact points).
- Lean on something: pew end, pillar, wall, table edge. Sit in a pew for vows and readings.
- 85mm: braced and static only. Never walk with it.
- 24mm walking: knees bent, heel-to-toe "ninja walk", hips not arms; IBIS Active (if present).
- Exhale slowly during critical shots; start recording 2 seconds before and stop 2 seconds after.
- 100p slow motion hides shake; use it for anything that moves.
- Post fallback: A7C writes gyro data; Catalyst Browse or Gyroflow can stabilize (*verify* support and whether IBIS must be off).

## 4. Lighting plan (available light only)

| Venue | Expect | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Church | Daylight windows (about 5600K) and warm tungsten or LED (2700–4000K) | Set WB to whichever light hits the couple's faces, usually about 4300–4800K. Shoot the couple with the windows to the side, not behind them. Expose for faces and let the windows clip slightly. Ask before Mass whether the overhead lights can be on throughout. |
| Restaurant | Unknown; probably warm LED downlights | Custom WB at arrival. Seat or frame people facing the brightest light. Avoid harsh downlights from directly above (eye shadows): step to the side. Use window seats for portraits if there are any. |
| Outdoors at noon | Harsh overhead sun (church steps, arrival) | Group photos in open shade by the building. With no ND, raise the shutter only for 100p shots (1/100); for 25p, stop down to f/8–f/11 rather than breaking 1/50. |

Golden and blue hour do not fall during the event. Approximate Dhaka times for 23 Oct: sunrise about 05:55, golden hour about 16:45–17:25, sunset about 17:25, blue hour about 17:25–17:50. **Confirm exact times with philip.** These only matter if the couple wants optional portraits later in the afternoon.
