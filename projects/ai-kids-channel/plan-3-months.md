# AI kids content · 3-month plan (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027)

Goal: build a Bangla kids channel (YouTube + Facebook) made with AI tools, and earn money in the first 3 months, mostly from **services** while the channel grows.

## Reality check first
- **YouTube ad money needs the YouTube Partner Program:** 1,000 subscribers **plus** either 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days (check the current numbers in YouTube Studio → Earn). Most new channels don't reach this in 3 months, so **don't count on ad income before month 3–6.**
- **YouTube doesn't pay for mass-produced AI videos.** Its "inauthentic content" policy demonetises repetitive, low-effort, template videos. To stay monetisable: your own scripts, your own voice, consistent original characters, real teaching value, and no re-uploads.
- **Kids content must be marked "Made for kids"** (a legal requirement). That means no personalised ads (lower earnings), no comments and no notifications. Bangladesh ad rates are also low. That's why the plan earns from services first.
- **Disclose AI** where the platform asks (realistic synthetic content), and never copy famous characters (Cocomelon, Peppa, Meena, etc.).

## The channel
**Working name ideas:** "গল্পের ঝুড়ি" · "ছোট্ট পাঠশালা" · "টুনটুনি টিভি" (check that the name is free on YouTube and Facebook)
**Audience:** Bangla-speaking children aged 3–8 and their parents, in Bangladesh and abroad. Bangla kids content is underserved compared with Hindi and English.

**Three content pillars**
1. **১ মিনিটের নীতিগল্প (Shorts):** Aesop, Panchatantra and Bangla folk tales retold with 2–3 original recurring characters (for example, a curious girl "টুনি" and her cat "মিঠু").
2. **শেখা:** অ আ ক খ, numbers, colours, fruits, animals and good habits. Evergreen; parents search for these.
3. **ছড়া ও গান:** only **public-domain** rhymes (traditional ছড়া; Sukumar Ray and Rabindranath are public domain in Bangladesh) or your own lyrics, with Suno music on a paid plan.

**Formats:** Shorts (30–60 s, 9:16) for reach, plus long videos (5–12 min, 16:9) made from Short compilations plus new linking scenes, for watch hours.

## Production pipeline (one Short ≈ 2–3 h once you're practised)
1. Script: you, or John drafts and you edit (Bangla, simple words, one lesson)
2. Character sheet: generate the same characters once and reuse them as reference images
3. Visuals: AI images, then Google Flow (Veo) or another image-to-video tool for movement
4. Voice: **your own Bangla voice** (DJI Mic Mini 2), or a clearly-licensed AI voice
5. Music: Suno (paid plan for commercial use) or the YouTube Audio Library
6. Edit in Premiere Pro: captions in large Bangla text, sound effects, colour
7. Thumbnail and title: bright, one character's face, 3–4 Bangla words

**Monthly tool budget (check current prices):** Google AI Pro for Flow ≈ $20, Suno Pro ≈ $10, about ৳4,000 a month in total. Start with the free tiers in week 1.

## Where the money comes from in 3 months
1. **Personalised kids videos for parents (fastest):** "আপনার সন্তানের নামে জন্মদিনের গল্প-ভিডিও". Sell on your Facebook page and in parent groups at ৳800–2,000 each. Each takes 1–2 h once you have templates.
2. **AI video gigs on Fiverr:** you already have the AI video gigs. Use the channel's best clips as portfolio.
3. **Kindergartens and schools in Dhaka:** short promo and admission videos, and animated lessons.
4. **YouTube and Facebook ads:** only after eligibility, probably from month 3–6.

## Week-by-week, month 1
- **Week 1:** pick the name, create the YouTube channel and Facebook page, design 2–3 characters, write 10 scripts, make 3 Shorts (learn the pipeline).
- **Week 2:** 5 Shorts, 1 sample personalised birthday video (using your own or a friend's child, with permission), post the offer on Facebook.
- **Week 3:** 6 Shorts + 1 long compilation, reply to every comment and message within 24 h.
- **Week 4:** 6 Shorts + 1 long; review analytics (which story got the most views and swipe-aways), double down on the winning format.

**Weekly time:** about 10–12 hours (batch-produce on 2 fixed days).

## What to check every Sunday (15 min)
- Views per Short and "viewed vs swiped away" (the hook)
- Average view duration on long videos
- New subscribers and followers
- Orders and income this week
- One thing to change next week

## Rules
- Nothing scary, violent or confusing for small children.
- Your own scripts and voice wherever possible; never re-upload others' content.
- Use public-domain stories and rhymes, or your own.
- Keep the same characters, intro and style so viewers recognise the channel.
