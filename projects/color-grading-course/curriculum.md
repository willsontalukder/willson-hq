# Colour grading in Premiere Pro · 30-day course for Frank

Goal: grade confidently in Premiere Pro (Lumetri Color) and recreate looks like the moody, earthy "Nany art" references.
Rhythm: one lesson + one hands-on task a day, about 45–60 min. Use your own footage (Banns of Marriage, Dhaka b-roll) whenever you can.
Progress is tracked in `progress.md`.

## Week 1 · See colour correctly (correction before grading)
1. Lumetri panel tour + scopes setup (Waveform, Vectorscope YUV, Parade RGB)
2. Exposure with the waveform: blacks, mids, highlights
3. White balance: eyedropper, Temperature/Tint, neutral greys on the Parade
4. Skin tones: the skin-tone line on the vectorscope
5. Contrast and saturation without clipping
6. Shot matching: Comparison View + Color Match
7. Review day: correct 5 clips from Friday's shoot, before/after stills

## Week 2 · The grading tools
8. RGB Curves: S-curve, lifted (milky) blacks, soft highlights
9. Hue Saturation Curves: Hue vs Sat, Hue vs Hue, Hue vs Luma
10. Color Wheels & Match: shadows / midtones / highlights
11. HSL Secondary: change one colour, protect skin
12. Creative tab: Faded Film, Vibrance, Shadow/Highlight Tint
13. Adjustment layers, saving presets, using LUTs responsibly
14. Review day: one 30 s sequence, corrected + one look

## Week 3 · Looks
15. Moody earthy forest look (the Nany art reference)
16. Warm golden-hour portrait look
17. Teal & orange (and when not to use it)
18. Clean commercial / product look (SamZone-style tech B-roll)
19. Black & white with contrast and tone
20. Film look: grain, softness, halation, vignette
21. Review day: one clip graded in 4 looks

## Week 4 · Real projects
22. Grade the Banns highlight (consistent look across a whole film)
23. Night and mixed light (LED, tungsten)
24. Skin on difficult light; fixing 8-bit banding
25. Documentary look for the Satkhira school film
26. Build your preset library (5 house looks)
27. Before/after Reel for Facebook/Instagram (content + portfolio)
28. Grade someone else's footage (practice gig)
29. Speed round: grade 10 clips in 30 minutes
30. Final project + self-review; decide whether to add a colour-grading gig on Fiverr

## Shooting for grading (from now on)
- For footage you plan to grade, use Creative Style **Neutral** (contrast −2, saturation −1, sharpness −2) or Picture Profile **PP10 (HLG2)**: flatter than Standard, still safe in 8-bit.
- Avoid S-Log3 on the A7C until you're comfortable: 8-bit S-Log3 needs exact exposure and bands easily.
- White balance by hand still matters: grading is easier when the base is right.
