# Look recipe · moody earthy forest (Nany art reference)

What the look is: low contrast, **milky lifted blacks**, warm **sepia/olive** tones, greens pushed toward **brown-olive**, cream highlights, natural warm skin, soft haze, gentle vignette and a little grain.

## 70% of this look is the shoot, not the grade
- **Fog or overcast**, or backlight in the last hour before sunset. No hard sun.
- **Earth-tone wardrobe:** cream, beige, linen, brown. Nothing bright or saturated.
- **Dark background** (forest, trees, grass) with the subject in a lighter, softer area.
- **85mm wide open**, lots of negative space, slow movement.
- Shoot flatter: Creative Style Neutral, contrast −2, saturation −1, so the grade has room.

## Premiere Pro: Lumetri Color, step by step
Work on an **adjustment layer** over the clips so the look stays consistent.

**1. Basic Correction (base)**
- White balance first (eyedropper), then Temperature **+5 to +10** (warmer)
- Contrast **−15**, Highlights **−25**, Shadows **+10**, Whites **−10**, Blacks **+5**
- Saturation **80**

**2. Curves → RGB**
- Lift the **black point** up to about **8–10%** (milky, faded blacks)
- Pull the **white point** down a little (about 95%): creamy highlights
- Very gentle S-curve in the mids

**3. Curves → Hue Saturation Curves**
- **Hue vs Hue:** grab green, shift it toward **yellow/olive**
- **Hue vs Sat:** green **−40%**, blue/cyan **−60%** (sky and cool tones almost disappear)
- **Hue vs Luma:** darken greens slightly so the forest goes deep and moody

**4. Color Wheels & Match**
- **Shadows:** nudge toward **orange-brown**, a little
- **Midtones:** very slight warm (yellow-orange)
- **Highlights:** toward **cream/yellow**, very slight

**5. Creative**
- **Faded Film: 15–25**
- **Vibrance: −10**
- **Shadow Tint:** olive/yellow-green, small; **Highlight Tint:** warm orange, small

**6. Vignette:** Amount **−1.0 to −1.5**, Midpoint **40**, Feather **70**

**7. Grain (optional):** Effects → **Noise** at about **3–5%** on a separate adjustment layer, or a 16mm grain overlay set to Overlay blend.

**8. Protect skin:** if faces go too yellow or grey, use **HSL Secondary** to select the skin, then add a touch of warmth and saturation back.

Save it as a preset: in the Lumetri panel ≡ menu → **Export .cube** or **Save Preset** as "KR Earthy Forest".
