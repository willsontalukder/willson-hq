# Day 1 · Lumetri panel + scopes (27 Oct)

## Lesson (10 min)
1. **Workspace:** Window → Workspaces → **Color**. On the right is the **Lumetri Color** panel; on the left, open **Lumetri Scopes** (Window → Lumetri Scopes).
2. **Scopes setup:** right-click inside Lumetri Scopes → turn on **Waveform (Luma)**, **Vectorscope YUV** and **Parade (RGB)**. Set the waveform to **IRE**.
3. **How to read them:**
   - **Waveform** = brightness from left to right of the frame. Bottom (0) = black, top (100) = white. Nothing should be squashed flat at 0 or 100 (that's lost detail).
   - **Parade** = the same thing split into Red, Green, Blue. If the bottoms of the three are at different heights in a grey/black area, there's a colour cast.
   - **Vectorscope** = colour and saturation. Further from the centre = more saturated. The thin line between yellow and red is the **skin-tone line**: faces should sit near it.
4. **Correction vs grading:** correction makes the shot *right* (exposure, white balance, matching). Grading makes it *feel* a certain way (the look). Always correct first.

## Task (30–40 min)
1. Import 3 clips from Friday's shoot: one in church, one outdoors, one at the restaurant.
2. For each clip, use **only Basic Correction**:
   - Use the **White Balance eyedropper** on something white or grey (wall, shirt, paper).
   - Set **Exposure** so the face sits around **55–70 IRE** on the waveform.
   - Adjust **Highlights/Whites** so nothing important is flat at 100, and **Shadows/Blacks** so the darkest part touches about 0–5.
3. Turn the Lumetri effect on and off (fx button) to compare before/after.
4. Export one before/after still per clip (File → Export → Frame, or Shift+E).
