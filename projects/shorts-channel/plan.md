# International Shorts channel · plan (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027)

## Decision
**Channel: "Frame by Frank"** (backup names: "Keep Rolling Films", "Rolling Frames", "Cut by Frank"). Check the @handle is free on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok before you register.
**Niche:** cinematic tricks and visual magic, made by a real filmmaker using camera **and** AI. English text and captions, little or no speech, so it works worldwide.

Why this niche:
- **International:** visual tricks need no language; creators, filmmakers and curious people watch them everywhere.
- **Monetisable:** your own footage and real editing skill count as authentic content. YouTube's "inauthentic content" rules target templated, repetitive, mass-produced AI videos, and from July 2026 also AI personas giving health or finance advice. A real filmmaker showing real work is the opposite of that.
- **Pays better than average:** creator, education and tech niches earn more per view than kids or pure entertainment.
- **Feeds your business:** every video is a portfolio piece for Fiverr, clients and the SamZone-type jobs, and the colour-grading course becomes content.

## The 5 formats (rotate them)
1. **AI vs Real:** "One of these is AI. Which one?" Your real A7C shot next to an AI version made in Flow, with the reveal at the end. A strong 2026 trend with high comments.
2. **Cheap vs Expensive:** phone vs A7C, ৳500 light vs window light, the same shot with bad vs good framing (the Perception Effect idea in 30 seconds).
3. **How I shot this:** the final cinematic shot first, then the BTS reveal (phone filming you), then the final again. Loops well.
4. **Before / after grade:** a flat clip turning into a look in 10–15 s, timed to a beat (made from your daily colour-grading lessons).
5. **10-second cinema:** a micro story shot in Dhaka (rain, tea stall, rickshaw lights, the river) with sound design and an ending that loops.

Rules for every Short: the hook in the first second (show the best frame first), 15–40 s, one idea, big English captions, loopable ending, and no copyrighted music (use YouTube's Audio Library or Shorts' own trending sounds).

## Monetisation: the main challenge
**Path to the YouTube Partner Program** (check the current numbers in YouTube Studio → Earn; sources in 2026 disagree):
- 1,000 subscribers **and** either 10 million Shorts views in 90 days **or** 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months.
- Shorts alone pay little (a few cents up to about $0.30 per 1,000 views), so we also build **watch hours with one long video a week**:
  - 6–10 minute tutorials: "How I shoot cinematic video alone", "Phone vs camera: full test", "Colour grade like this in Premiere Pro".
  - 4,000 hours ≈ 60,000 views of an 8-minute video at 50% retention. That's realistic over 6–9 months with Shorts sending viewers to the long videos.

**Money before YPP:**
1. Fiverr and local clients: link the channel as your portfolio (fastest money).
2. Sell a preset/LUT pack ("Frame by Frank Earthy Forest") once the course reaches Week 3: $5–10 on Gumroad or Ko-fi.
3. Affiliate links for gear you really use (camera, mic, lens) on Amazon or B&H, where they accept Bangladesh-based creators; check eligibility.
4. Brand deals: phone, camera and editing-app brands look for exactly this kind of creator once you have 10k+ followers.

**Monetisation safety rules:**
- Every video has your own footage or real editing work in it; AI is one ingredient, never the whole video.
- No re-uploads, no reaction to others' clips, no template you repeat 50 times.
- Tag realistic AI content with YouTube's AI label and say "AI" in the AI-vs-Real reveals.
- Post the same Shorts on Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook Reels (more reach and more income chances), removing watermarks before posting.

## Posting rhythm
- **5 Shorts + 1 long video a week.** Batch-produce on 2 fixed days.
- **Post at about 8:00 AM Dhaka time** (about 9–10 PM US East the evening before, and early morning in Europe).
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.
