## 2. Packages and gig extras

Rules that apply to every gig:
- "Raw footage in" is the maximum client footage Frank has to review. Past that limit, the "extra raw footage" add-on applies.
- Music: either the client supplies licensed tracks, or Frank uses a track from a library the client licenses. Frank does **not** resell his own music licence.
- Accepted inputs: H.264/H.265/ProRes up to 4K. Camera RAW or 6K+ footage gets a quote first. The laptop's GPU is unknown, so proxies add time.
- Delivery: H.264 MP4. ProRes is available on request at no charge, if file size allows.
- "Revision" means one round of consolidated notes on the same cut. A new direction is a new order.

## 3. Fiverr's fee and Frank's net

**Seller fee: 20% of every order, including gig extras and tips.** Frank keeps 80%. This was checked against several 2026 third-party sources, which all agree: [hireinsouth.com (2026)](https://hireinsouth.com/post/fiverr-pricing), [vaultleap.com (2026)](https://vaultleap.com/blog/fiverr-fees-explained-2026), [nethustler.com fee calculator (2026)](https://nethustler.com/tools/fiverr-fee-calculator/) and [worldfirst.com](https://www.worldfirst.com/sasia/blog/freelancer-payments/get-paid-on-fiverr-world-account-south-asia/). **Flag:** Fiverr's own Help Center page could not be opened from here (DNS blocked). Frank should check it once at Fiverr → Help → "Fiverr fees for sellers". Buyers separately pay a 5.5% service fee plus a small-order fee. That fee does not reduce Frank's share, but it does make his gig look more expensive to the buyer.

Other cash-flow points, also from third-party sources:
- **Clearance:** funds become available 14 days after an order completes, or 7 days for Top Rated / Pro / Seller Plus Premium ([worldfirst.com](https://www.worldfirst.com/sasia/blog/freelancer-payments/get-paid-on-fiverr-world-account-south-asia/)). Early Payout costs about 1% ([wise.com](https://wise.com/us/blog/how-to-get-paid-on-fiverr)).
- **Withdrawal:** Payoneer charges about $1 (2-day) or $3 (2-hour) per withdrawal ([wise.com US](https://wise.com/us/blog/how-to-get-paid-on-fiverr), [wise.com UK](https://wise.com/gb/blog/fiverr-payments)). Sources conflict on the exact figure, and Bangladesh-specific fees are not confirmed. Batch withdrawals so the fee stays under 1%. It is left out of the per-package figures below.
- Tax on freelance income in Bangladesh was **not researched** here. Treat it as an open item for a tax adviser.

**Floors to protect:**
- **Do not accept under $10/h net (~৳1,220/h).** At that point a Fiverr order earns less than a local shoot once Fiverr's 20% and the 14-day wait are counted.
- **Watch the scope creep spots.** Wedding Basic and YouTube Basic are the thinnest margins. If a buyer sends more footage than the package allows, charge the "extra raw footage" add-on rather than absorbing it.
- **Render time** on the TUF laptop is unknown. If 4K exports tie up the machine for hours, deliveries should be scheduled overnight rather than the hours counted at a lower rate.
- **Custom offers** (Fiverr's quote feature) work out from hours × $12–15 net, then ÷ 0.8 to get the price to quote. Example: 8 h × $13 = $104 net, so quote ~$130.

## 5. Pricing ladder

Fiverr's level requirements, from [Fiverr Help Center "Achieving levels"](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010560118-Achieving-levels) as seen in a search snippet (undated). Third-party guides ([fiverrtutorials.com](https://fiverrtutorials.com/fiverr-level-one), [zenlance.net](https://zenlance.net/fiverr-seller-levels/)) disagree on some of the extra conditions, such as the number of active days and Level 1's rating minimum, so Frank should check his own Fiverr dashboard.

| Level | Success Score | Rating | Response | Orders | Unique clients | Earnings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | 5+ | 4.4+ | 80% | 5 | 3 | $400 |
| Level 2 | 7+ | 4.6+ | 90% | 20 | 10 | $2,000 |
| Top Rated | 9+ | 4.7+ | 90% | 40 | 20 | $10,000 + manual review |

| Stage | Trigger | Action | Net target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Launch** | 0–4 reviews | Use the prices in this document. Do not discount Standard or Premium. Basic is the "door" that brings first orders. If there are no orders after ~30 days with healthy impressions, cut **Basic only** by $5 (short-form to $20, YouTube to $40). Never go below $10/h net. | $10–12/h |
| **1. First proof** | **5 reviews at 4.8+** or Level 1 | Raise Basic ~20% (e.g. YouTube $55, short-form $30). Raise Standard/Premium ~10% (YouTube $105/$200, wedding $165/$320). | $12–14/h |
| **2. Established** | **20 reviews** or Level 2 | +20–25% on all tiers (e.g. YouTube $65/$130/$240, short-form $35/$85/$200, wedding $90/$190/$360). Add a 4-week "repeat client" custom-offer bundle. | $15–17/h |
| **3. In demand** | 40+ reviews, Top Rated, or Pro | Price towards the top of the observed market: wedding $250+ for a 3–5 min highlight, colorist $100–240. Drop the cheapest Basic or make it a "1 Short" sampler. | $20–25/h |

Raise prices at any stage if any of these happens:
- The order queue stays over 7 days of work for 2 weeks running.
- More than 1 in 3 buyer inquiries converts. That suggests the price is too low.
- Real hours from the time log are 25% or more above the estimates in §4.

How to raise:
- Raise one gig at a time, and wait about 2 weeks before the next.
- Honour the old price for repeat buyers for one month by sending them custom offers.
- Never raise in the middle of an order.
