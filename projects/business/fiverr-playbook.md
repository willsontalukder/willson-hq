## 2. The three gigs to run (ranked)

### Why these three

What the research shows, and how firm each finding is:

- **Short-form demand is real.** Fiverr's own Fall 2025 Business Trends Index (released 10 Dec 2025) reports a 66% rise in
  demand for AI video creators over six months. It also ties growth in platform-specific services to short-form,
  engagement-driven content ([Fiverr news](https://www.fiverr.com/news/2025-fall-business-trends-index); [AOL syndication](https://lite.aol.com/entertainment/story/0022/20251210/9600016.htm)).
  We found **no Fiverr figure specific to video editing** and no 2026 edition of the index. The "AI video" number is
  about AI-generated video, which Frank doesn't sell. Read it as a sign that short-form video budgets are growing, not as
  proof of editing demand.
- **Generic Reels gigs are crowded.** Live listings show many near-identical "I will edit reels with captions" gigs,
  many of them from South Asian sellers with large review counts. Niche gigs stand out: coaches and course creators,
  fitness, real estate, talking-head editing ([examples](https://fiverr.com/jam_abid/script-edit-reels-coaches),
  [examples](https://fiverr.com/jamie_deeps/edit-short-form-content)). **(Our reading of the listings, not market data.)**
- **Colour grading is crowded too, but specialised.** One Fiverr category page showed about 15,943 "color" services.
  Log → Rec.709, S-Log3 and LUT work are standard offerings ([block.fiverr.com/gigs/color](https://block.fiverr.com/gigs/color?page=13);
  [example gig](https://fiverr.com/ajoshuafilms/do-professional-cinematic-color-grading-davinci-resolve)). No demand data was
  found. **(Unverified demand.)**
- **Wedding highlight editing is an active market.** Listings start around $80–125 and deliver in 3–7 days for
  highlights, 7–14 for full films ([block.fiverr.com wedding-highlight](https://block.fiverr.com/gigs/wedding-highlight);
  [example](https://fiverr.com/nitheesh_seban/do-professional-wedding-reels)). We found no data on how many buyers
  there are. It needs hours of footage per order (large uploads from abroad) and long edit sessions, so it's a poor
  first gig for a solo editor who also shoots local weddings.

**Ranking logic for Frank:** pick the gigs where (a) buyers come back every week or month, (b) one order fits around
local shoots, and (c) Frank's "I shoot too" story is a real edge. Drone editing has too few buyers to stand alone
**(unverified; no data found)**, so it goes into Gig 3 as a selling point and a tag.

## 3. 30-day Fiverr growth routine

Built for one person who also shoots. **About 30–40 minutes a day**, plus one longer session a week.

### Every day (≈30 min)

- **Reply within 1 hour** to every message during your working hours. Keep the Fiverr app open with notifications on.
  Even on shoot days, a quick "Got it, I'm on a shoot and will reply properly by 8 pm Dhaka time" counts.
- **Check Briefs** (if your account receives them) and reply to every one that fits within the same day. Briefs are
  sent by Fiverr's matching to a small group of sellers, not posted to an open board ([Fiverr help: Briefs](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/4415608857745);
  [Fiverr community: brief tips](https://community.fiverr.com/public/blogs/nail-your-brief-responses-with-these-few-tips-2025-06-02)).
  One guide says ignored invitations may be tracked **(unverified)** ([AiProposer](https://aiproposer.com/guides/fiverr/fiverr-buyer-request-responses)).
  If a brief doesn't fit, decline it rather than ignoring it.
- **Deliver every order on time**, ideally a few hours early. A late order costs more than a missed brief.

### How to write a good Brief reply (≈5 min each)

Use this structure. Write it fresh each time, and never send a copy-paste:

1. **Mirror their goal in one line.** "You need 8 Reels a month from your coaching videos, with captions in your brand colours."
2. **Show one relevant sample.** Link the single portfolio piece closest to their request, not the whole gallery.
3. **Say exactly what you'll do.** 3 bullets: cut, captions, colour, delivery time.
4. **Ask one smart question** that shows you read the brief. "Are the videos filmed on a phone or a camera?"
5. **Make a clear offer** on-platform: package/price **[Matthew's price]**, delivery date, revisions.
6. **Stop.** Under ~120 words. No "Dear Sir", no list of every skill, no begging, no contact details.

Example:
> Hi Sarah, you want your weekly coaching videos turned into 8 Reels a month, with captions in your brand colours and
> a fast hook. Here's a talking-head Reel I edited and graded: [portfolio link on Fiverr]. I'd cut each to under 45 s,
> open on your strongest line, add word-by-word captions and correct the colour. Delivery: first 4 in 4 days. Quick
> question: do you film on a phone or a camera? Offer attached: 8 Reels, 2 revisions each, **[Matthew's price]**.
> — Frank

### Weekly (one 60–90 min session, e.g. every Saturday)

- **Week 1. Fix the foundations.** Run the §1 checklist. Rewrite the profile. Publish or rework Gigs 1–3 with the copy
  above. Make the 3 gig videos (20–60 s each, original footage, licensed music only). Make the before/after image 1 for
  each gig.
- **Week 2. Portfolio and proof.** Add 6–10 original portfolio pieces. Cut 2 before/after grade Reels from your own
  shoots (they double as FB content). Post the first FB gig promo (below).
- **Week 3. Look at the numbers.** In Analytics, note impressions → clicks → orders per gig. Lots of impressions but few
  clicks: fix gig image 1 and the title. Clicks but no orders: fix the description, pricing and FAQs. Change one thing
  per gig, then leave it alone. Write the numbers into this file's log at the bottom.
- **Week 4. Repeat buyers and review check.** Message every past buyer once, on Fiverr only, with something useful
  (see below). Re-run the §1 checklist. Send Jude the screenshots for a proper audit.

### Turning buyers into repeat buyers

- Ask at delivery, never before: *"If you post weekly, I can hold a regular slot for you. Want me to send a custom
  offer for next month?"* Then send it **as a Fiverr custom offer**.
- Keep a short style note per buyer (fonts, colours, caption style, music mood) in your own files, so order 2 needs no
  briefing. That consistency is what makes buyers come back.
- Upsell honestly across the gigs. YouTube buyer → "want 3 Shorts cut from this?" Grade buyer → "want the LUT?"
- One check-in 2–3 weeks after delivery, on Fiverr: "Your Reel's been up a couple of weeks. Want the next batch
  in the same style?" One message, no chasing.

### Asking for a review, honestly

Fiverr bans manipulating reviews: fake reviews, arranged or "strategic" orders to boost ratings, multiple accounts,
and pressuring anyone for positive feedback ([Fiverr: Integrity and Authenticity](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243087473553-Integrity-and-Authenticity);
[Fiverr: Account restrictions](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/34551010553489-Account-restrictions)).
Third-party sources disagree on whether a plain request is allowed. Community posts say a polite request for an
*honest* review is fine **(unverified: we couldn't read Fiverr's own wording)** ([Fiverr community thread](https://community.fiverr.com/public/forum/boards/start-here-c9d/posts/311345-can-we-ask-buyers-to-write-a-review-it-is-not-against-tos)).
Safe approach: **one** neutral line in the delivery message, nothing more.

> "Here's your final video. If anything needs changing, just tell me. If you're happy with the work, an honest
> review on Fiverr helps a solo editor like me a lot. Thank you for trusting me with your footage."

Never: offer a discount, extra or freebie for a review; ask for "5 stars"; ask a buyer to change or remove a review;
hold back files until they review; ask for a review a second time.

### Promoting gigs off-platform (Facebook page "Keep Rolling", 581 followers)

Fiverr (§4).

## 4. Fiverr rules to respect

These protect the account. Breaking them can get it restricted or suspended, which would cost more than any order.

| Rule | What it means for Frank | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **No fake, traded or arranged reviews** | No review swaps with other sellers, no friends or family ordering to leave reviews, no second accounts, no "order my $5 gig and I'll refund you". | [Integrity and Authenticity](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243087473553-Integrity-and-Authenticity); [Account restrictions](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/34551010553489-Account-restrictions) |
| **No pressure for feedback** | One neutral review request at delivery, that's it. Never pressure a buyer about their rating. | [Integrity and Authenticity](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243087473553-Integrity-and-Authenticity) |
| **No paying off-platform** | Never suggest bKash, PayPal, bank transfer, a direct contract or "cheaper outside Fiverr" to anyone you met on Fiverr. All payments go through Fiverr. | [Off-platform policy](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/38728907371665-Community-Standards-Off-platform-policy) |
| **No contact details in Fiverr messages** | No phone number, email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram handle or Facebook link in messages, gig text, gig images or the gig video. Footage links (Drive/Dropbox/WeTransfer) for the work itself are normal practice. | [Off-platform policy](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/38728907371665-Community-Standards-Off-platform-policy); [Stay protected](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/12792122691601-Communication-The-Dos-and-Don-ts) |
| **Keep communication on Fiverr** | One Fiverr help article suggests third-party tools may be OK *after* an order, if you document them back on Fiverr. A Fiverr community post says moving to WhatsApp/Discord isn't allowed. **Sources conflict, so take the safe reading: everything on Fiverr.** If a buyer asks for a call, use Fiverr's own call feature if your account has it. | [Stay protected](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/12792122691601-Communication-The-Dos-and-Don-ts); [Fiverr community blog](https://community.fiverr.com/public/blogs/taking-business-off-platform-2025-06-02) |
| **Original portfolio only** | Only work you shot and/or edited yourself. For client work, get the client's OK before showing it. No stock showreels, no other editors' work, no AI-generated "samples" presented as your edits. Gig video: no copyrighted music or visuals you don't own, no watermarks. | [Creating a Gig](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010451397-Creating-a-Gig); [Integrity and Authenticity](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243087473553-Integrity-and-Authenticity) |
| **Honest claims** | Don't claim views, client names, years or review counts you can't prove. The copy above deliberately has none. Add real numbers only once they're true. | [Integrity and Authenticity](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243087473553-Integrity-and-Authenticity) |
