## 3. Raising rates with a low-paying client (e.g. Roboshikhun)

1. **Know your number first.** Log real hours on the next 2 videos. Hours × ৳700 = the price you need. If their current
   rate is less than half of that, raise in two steps (halfway now, full price in 3 months) so the jump is not a shock.
2. **Give notice, not surprises.** New rates start on a date about 30 days away (**1 Dec 2026**). Jobs already sent
   finish at the old rate. Never raise mid-job.
3. **Give a choice and a fence.** Per-video price, or the cheaper monthly package (4 for ৳10,000). Write the scope limits
   (footage max, 2 revisions, captions extra). If they push back, **cut scope, not rate**: e.g. ৳2,500 for 2 min, 1
   revision, no captions.

## 5. Free legal tools until then

- **CapCut Desktop (free):** use it now on 8 GB for Reels and quick captions. Free exports can carry a watermark if a
  Pro-only asset is used, and 4K may be locked; avoid Pro items. Don't use its music in client work until the track's
  licence for commercial use is confirmed.
- **DaVinci Resolve (free):** use it after the RAM upgrade for 16:9 edits like testimonials. A third-party guide gives 16 GB
  RAM as the minimum, so it will struggle on 8 GB. The free version does H.264/H.265 decoding on the CPU, so make
  proxies. This is a stopgap only: Premiere stays Frank's main tool (`team/studio.md`), and Resolve projects won't open in Premiere.
