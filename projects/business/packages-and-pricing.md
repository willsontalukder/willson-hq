## 2. The packages

All packages: one shooter (Frank), Sony A7C with 24mm and 85mm primes, DJI Mic Mini 2 wireless audio for speeches/vows/
talking-to-camera, natural and available light (**no lighting kit, gimbal or tripod owned**: said up front so nobody
expects a multi-camera studio setup). Licensed music only. Colour grading included. Files delivered by Google Drive link
(kept online 30 days; client downloads and keeps their own copy).

**Drone: not sold yet.** Bangladesh's drone rules are unsettled. The 2020 policy requires written permission (older
reporting says 45 days in advance) ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/109795), [DFDL](https://dfdl.com/publication/drones-regulatory-framework-in-bangladesh-top-5-key-qas)).
A 2024 draft would exempt only sub-250 g drones **without** camera/recording ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/new-drone-regulations-draft-licence-and-clearance-be-mandatory-3751206)),
so a camera drone would not qualify. CAAB banned drone flights near airports from Dec 2025 and nationwide until 15 Feb
2026 ([Bangladesh Monitor](https://www.bangladeshmonitor.com.bd/lead-news-pdf/drone-flights-banned-nationwide-until-february-15)),
and draft Civil Aviation Rules 2026 are out for consultation ([Bangladesh Monitor](https://bangladeshmonitor.com.bd/en/govt-drafts-new-aviation-rulebook-ahead-of-icao-audit)).
Dhaka is densely populated and close to the airport. **Rule:** the drone add-on stays off every quote until Simon confirms
with CAAB what is allowed for a specific location and date, and the permission is in writing. The ৳5,000 price is
deliberately below studio drone add-ons (FrameCipher ৳25,000) because the Neo 2 is a small drone with limited shot types.

### Travel note (all packages)

- **Included:** anywhere inside Dhaka North and Dhaka South City Corporation areas.
- **Outside Dhaka city** (e.g. Savar, Gazipur, Narayanganj, Purbachal): client pays actual return transport (CNG/Uber/bus
  receipt) **plus ৳1,000 for each hour of travel time beyond 1 hour each way**.
- **Out of town / overnight:** client arranges and pays transport, accommodation and meals; quoted case by case.
- Venue entry fees, parking or permits (if any) are paid by the client.

## 3. Terms (put the short version on every quote)

1. **Booking & advance:** **50% advance** locks the date. Pay by **bKash** (Send Money to Frank's personal number, or his
   merchant/payment number once he has one) or **bank transfer**. Client pays any sending charge so Frank receives the full
   amount. Date is not held until the advance arrives; first paid advance wins a date.
2. **Balance:** remaining 50% **on delivery**. Frank shares a watermarked or low-resolution preview first; full-quality
   files are released when the balance is paid. Payment due within 3 days of the final cut.
3. **Revisions: 2 rounds included.** One consolidated list of changes per round, sent within 14 days of each delivery.
   Revisions cover changes to the existing edit (order, trims, text, music choice from the licensed library). New footage,
   a new concept or a reshoot is a new quote. If no feedback arrives in 14 days, the delivery counts as approved.
4. **Cancellation / postponement by client:**
   - **A (events):** 30+ days before the date: advance can move to a new date within 6 months, or 50% of the advance is
     refunded. Under 30 days: advance is non-refundable, but one free date change if Frank is available.
   - **B, C:** more than 72 h before the shoot: advance moves to a new date. Under 72 h: advance is kept.
5. **Cancellation by Frank** (illness, emergency): full refund of everything paid, or a new date if the client prefers.
   Frank will also try to recommend another videographer.
6. **Hartal, severe weather, venue closure, government restrictions:** free reschedule, no penalty either side.
7. **Usage rights:**
   - **A:** the couple/family get a permanent personal licence: share online, with family, at events. Not for commercial
     resale.
   - **B, C:** the business gets a licence to use the videos on its own social pages and website, with no time limit.
     **Boosted/paid ads:** tell Frank before booking so the music can be licensed for ads. TV, cinema, billboard or reselling the footage to another brand: ask first (separate fee).
   - Music: every track is licensed through Frank's [library name, plan] for use in this video on the channels listed
     above. Please don't replace the music, use the track on its own, or re-edit the video with other music; ask Frank
     instead. If a platform shows a copyright notice, send Frank a screenshot and he will clear it.
     *(James-the-less confirms the library and tier covers client work, paid ads and use after the subscription ends
     before the first B/C quote.)*
   - **Frank keeps the copyright** and may use clips in his portfolio and showreel. **For weddings, only with the
     couple's written OK** (sales-plan ground rule); the couple can say no and it doesn't change the price.
8. **Files & backup:** delivered files stay online 30 days. Frank keeps the project archive for 6 months as a courtesy,
   not a guarantee. Clients should keep their own copy.
9. **What's not included:** lighting kit, gimbal, second camera operator, drone (unless confirmed legal), printed/USB
   delivery, live streaming.

**For Frank (not on the quote):** bKash cash-out costs about **৳14.90–18.50 per ৳1,000** (1.49%–1.85%), depending on
agent type and monthly limits ([bKash FAQ](https://www.bkash.com/en/page/rem-faq), [Observer, June 2026](https://observerbd.com/news/564792));
sources disagree on the exact limits, so check the bKash app. Keep money in bKash or move it to the bank instead of
cashing out where you can. **Taxes:** I have not researched Bangladesh's freelance income tax for Frank's situation and
won't guess a rate. Default: put **10% of every payment aside** in a separate account until a tax adviser or NBR guidance
confirms what's owed.

**Reading the numbers:**
- At intro prices Frank earns roughly **৳490–575 per real hour**. The only comparable hourly BDT figure found is a
  ৳600/h mobile video-editing listing on Bikroy. So intro prices are a **portfolio rate, not a profit rate**. That's
  why the intro window is limited (first 3 bookings per package or until 28 Feb 2027, whichever is first), then
  standard prices apply (~৳670–750/h).
- **Editing is the cost driver.** On A, Frank works ~8 hours for every 1 hour on site. The biggest lever is a reusable

- **The B monthly plan** is the best cash-flow product: lower rate per job but no hunting cost and predictable income.
