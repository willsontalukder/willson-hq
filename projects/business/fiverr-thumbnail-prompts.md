# Fiverr gig thumbnails · prompts

For Frank's three live gigs. One look across all three so the profile reads as one brand:
**Keep Rolling colours**: near-black background, warm gold (#E8B54A-ish) headline, one small red REC dot.

## How to make each thumbnail

1. **Background / scene:** generate it with the AI prompt below (ChatGPT, Gemini or Ideogram), or better, use a real frame from your own footage.
2. **Your face:** use your **real photo** (the new profile photo), not an AI face. Buyers trust a real person, and Fiverr wants gig images to show your own work and identity.
3. **Text:** add it yourself in Canva or Photoshop. AI tools often misspell words in images.
4. **Size:** 1280 × 769 px (Fiverr's recommended gig image size; check it in the gig editor). Keep text and your face inside the middle 80%, because Fiverr crops the corners on small cards.
5. **Rules:** no other people's faces, no celebrity or stock faces made to look like clients, no "click bait" wording, no fake view counts or logos of brands you haven't worked with.

## Gig 1 · UGC / TikTok / Reels editing

**Headline:** `SCROLL-STOPPING REELS` · small line: `UGC · TikTok · Reels · Shorts`

**Layout:** you on the right third holding a phone, looking at camera; two or three vertical 9:16 phone frames on the left showing bold captions.

**AI background prompt:**
```
Dark cinematic studio background for a video-editing thumbnail, 16:10 landscape. On the left, three floating vertical smartphone screens in a slight fan, each showing a bright lifestyle short-video frame (coffee pour, skincare product, person talking) with bold white captions and colourful subtitle highlights. Soft warm gold rim light, subtle film grain, deep black and charcoal tones, one small red recording dot glowing in the top left. Empty clean space on the right third for a person. No text, no logos, no watermark, no faces in the foreground.
```

**Using your own photo (image-edit tools that accept an upload):**
```
Using my uploaded photo as the person, place me on the right third of a 1280x769 landscape thumbnail, waist up, holding a smartphone and smiling at the camera. Keep my face exactly as in the photo, natural skin, no beauty filter. Background: dark charcoal studio with warm gold rim light and three floating vertical phone screens on the left showing short-form video frames with bold captions. Leave the top left clear for a headline. No text, no logos.
```

## Gig 2 · YouTube video editing

**Headline:** `YOUTUBE EDITING THAT KEEPS VIEWERS` · small line: `Cuts · Captions · B-roll · Sound`

**Layout:** split frame. Left: a raw, messy timeline ("before"). Right: a polished YouTube-style frame ("after"). You small in the corner, or skip your face and let the before/after sell it.

**AI background prompt:**
```
Clean professional thumbnail for a YouTube video editing service, 16:10 landscape. A wide desk monitor showing a video editing timeline with many colourful clips, audio waveforms and captions, in a dark editing room with warm gold desk light and a soft blue monitor glow. Shallow depth of field, cinematic, modern, tidy. The right half of the screen shows a bright, polished talking-head video frame. Subtle film grain, deep blacks. No readable text, no software logos, no watermark.
```

**Tip:** a real screenshot of your own Premiere Pro timeline is stronger than an AI one. Blur any client footage you don't have permission to show.

## Gig 3 · Wedding & event highlight editing

**Headline:** `YOUR WEDDING, CINEMATIC` · small line: `Highlight film · Teaser · Premiere Pro`

**Layout:** one emotional wide frame (couple, warm light) across the whole image, a thin film-strip of 3 smaller moments along the bottom, headline top left in gold.

**AI background prompt:**
```
Cinematic wedding film still for a video editing thumbnail, 16:10 landscape. A South Asian bride in an ivory saree and a groom in a dark suit seen from behind, walking hand in hand out of a church doorway into soft golden light, flower petals in the air, warm film tones, gentle lens flare, shallow depth of field. Along the bottom edge, a thin horizontal film strip with three small frames: rings on an open book, a laughing couple, a tiered wedding cake. Deep shadows on the left for a headline. No text, no logos, no watermark, faces not clearly identifiable.
```

**Best version:** once the Banns couple agrees in writing, use a real frame from Friday instead of the AI image. Real wedding work sells this gig far better.

## Text style (all three)

- Headline font: a tall condensed bold (e.g. Bebas Neue or Big Shoulders Display), gold on black, 2 lines max.
- Small line: clean sans (e.g. Inter or IBM Plex Sans), white at 80%.
- A small "Keep Rolling" logo in one corner, same corner on all three gigs.
- No emojis, arrows or "16x more views" style claims.
