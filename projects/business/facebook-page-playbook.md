   the most-shared post visible. That supports the brand line in the content calendar: one filmmaker, one camera.

## 3. Organic reach that's honest

What actually helps a local service Page grow without ads:

1. **Post on a rhythm.** 3–4 times a week (the calendar), at the same evening hours (8–10:30 PM, plus Fri/Sat
   afternoon). Monthly posting is the main reason reach is low now.
2. **Lead with Reels, made natively vertical.** Short vertical video is where Facebook shows Pages to people who don't
   follow them yet. Burn in Bangla + English subtitles; most people scroll with sound off.
3. **Original work only.** Meta has said it favours original content and reduces reach for reposted or recycled
   material. Your own footage is your advantage; AI-generated visuals are not (see the Oct 14 post).
4. **Make the first line do the work.** The hook is the first line of every caption above, because only that shows
   before "See more".
5. **Make it worth sharing.** Your posts already get shared more than liked. Practical local tips (the 50 Hz flicker
   fix) and moving moments get shared; plain "I'm available" posts don't. Keep the sales posts few and useful.
6. **Reply to every comment and message within a few hours.** Conversations under a post help it travel, and a fast
   reply wins bookings. 15 minutes a day (sales plan).
7. **Help in Facebook groups, don't spam them.** Answer lighting and video questions in wedding, food and small-business
   groups; only post your own work where the group rules allow promotion.
8. **Work with other vendors.** Tag decorators, makeup artists, venues and cafés you worked with (with their OK) and
   share their posts. Their followers are exactly your clients.
9. **Invite people who react to like the Page,** if your Page shows that option on a post's reactions list. They already
   know your work.
10. **Share new posts to your own WhatsApp status and personal profile** once, to people who know you.
11. **Check Insights every two weeks** and judge by shares, saves, messages and enquiries, not likes. Move posting
    times to what your own audience shows.

**Never do (it breaks Meta's rules, hurts reach, or damages trust):**
- Buy likes, followers, views or reviews, or join like-for-like / follow-for-follow groups.
- Engagement bait: "Comment YES", "Tag 5 friends", "Share to win", fake polls. Meta reduces reach for this. Genuine
  questions ("24mm or 85mm?") are fine.
- Fake scarcity ("last 2 dates!") or fake testimonials.
- Mass or automated messages, or adding people to groups without asking.
- Posting a client's faces or footage without their OK.
