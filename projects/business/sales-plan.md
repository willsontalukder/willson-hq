# Sales Plan · Client hunting · 21 Oct – 20 Dec 2026 (60 days)

Owner: Peter (producer) · For: Frank, solo · Status: **DRAFT. Frank sends every message and approves every post himself.**

**Goal in one sentence:** turn Frank's Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/share/1XdgP8j57k/) into a steady source
of paid enquiries and land the first paid jobs by 20 Dec 2026, with no ad budget assumed.
We could not open the page, so its name, followers and posts are **unknown**. Jude audits it once Frank shares a
screenshot or admin access. No stats in this plan are real numbers about his page.

## 1. 60-day targets (targets, not facts)

| Metric | Target by 20 Dec |

Defaults chosen: no paid ads in the first 30 days (revisit with Matthew on 20 Nov); bookings need a deposit

written OK** (see content-calendar.md ground rule 1).

## 2. Target niches, ranked

1. **Weddings, holud, engagement.** Dec–Feb is peak season and couples book now. Biggest ticket per job, and the
   Banns film (23 Oct) is fresh proof. Frank's solo, discreet style suits small/intimate events that big studios
   overcharge for. Risk: big weddings may need a second shooter; quote solo-friendly events only.
2. **Restaurants and cafés (Dhanmondi, Gulshan, Banani).** They need Reels every month, so one client can repeat.
   Shoots are short (1–2 h), daytime, near home. 85mm food/detail shots and 1080p 100p slow-mo show well.
   Warm lead: Green Garden Restaurant (lunch venue 23 Oct), only if Frank films some food b-roll with the
   manager's OK.
3. **Small online shops (Facebook/Instagram brands).** Product videos can be shot at home by window light, any day,
   so they fill gaps between events. Lower price, faster cash, good for portfolio variety.

Later (after 60 days): event venues, real-estate walk-throughs, corporate. Drone work only where Simon confirms it is legal.

## 3. The offer: 3 starter packages (Matthew prices in BDT)

- **A. Intimate Wedding / Holud / Engagement:** one shooter, up to ~5 h, 3–5 min highlight film + 60 s vertical teaser.
- **B. Restaurant/Café Reels:** one ~2 h shoot → 3 vertical Reels (15–30 s) with captions; optional monthly repeat.
- **C. Product Starter:** up to 3 products → 1 hero video (30–45 s) + 3 short cuts for Reels/ads.

Every package states: delivery time, revisions (two rounds), licensed music only, 50% advance to book, balance on delivery.

## 4. The funnel

1. **Facebook page** (+ Reels) shows proof: pinned showreel, packages post, reviews, clear "Send WhatsApp message" button.
2. **Messenger / WhatsApp:** reply within a few hours with a saved, friendly first reply that asks date, location,
   event type and budget range. WhatsApp Business app (free) for labels and quick replies.
3. **Quote:** Matthew's template, sent as a short PDF or message within 24 h of the chat.
4. **Booking:** written confirmation + deposit received = date locked. Then hand off to Peter for the job plan.
5. **After delivery:** ask for a Facebook recommendation and a referral; permission to post a clip.

## 6. Lead sources in Dhaka

- **Facebook groups:** wedding planning, bride/groom, food-lover and small-business groups. Read each group's rules;
  only post where promo is allowed; mostly help in comments (lighting/flicker tips) so people come to the page.
- **Wedding planners, decorators, makeup artists, photographers** without video: referral partners, not one-off clients.
- **Restaurants and cafés** in Dhanmondi, Gulshan, Banani, especially new openings and ones posting weak Reels.
- **Instagram cafés** and small **online shops** with good products but phone-quality videos.
- **Event venues** and convention halls: ask to be on their list of recommended videographers.
- **Warm network:** friends, the Banns couple's circle (only if they offer to refer), past classmates.

**Ethics (non-negotiable):** no spam, no bought followers or reviews, no mass or automated messages, no adding people
to groups without asking. Each message names the business and one real observation. Frank sends everything himself.
If someone says no or doesn't reply after one follow-up, stop.
