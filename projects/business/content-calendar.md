## 1. Brand in one line

**"One filmmaker. One camera. Real Dhaka moments."**
Frank's edge is that he does it **alone** and it still looks cinematic. Every post should prove one of three
things: (a) the films are beautiful, (b) he's calm and capable working solo, (c) he knows his craft (tips).

**Consistent style (keep it the same on every post):**
- **Look:** warm, natural skin tones, soft contrast, no heavy teal-orange. The look is baked in on the camera
  (same in-camera look on every shoot), so there's no grading step: what he films is what gets posted.
- **Lenses as signature:** 24mm for place, 85mm for faces. Mention it often; it's memorable and true.
- **Text on screen:** one clean font, white with a soft shadow, lower third. Bangla + English captions
  burned in (most people scroll with sound off).
- **Voice:** friendly, a little Banglish, never salesy. Talk like a filmmaker friend, not an ad.
- **Handle (suggested, check availability):** `@frankwillson.films` everywhere, same profile photo
  (Frank with the A7C, 85mm portrait), same bio:
  > Wedding & event films · Dhaka
  > One-person crew · Sony A7C · 24mm + 85mm
  > Booking 2026–27 → WhatsApp/DM

  under the same LED), the settings, one line on PAL mode. CTA: "Save this before your next indoor shoot."
- **Footage:** G flicker test grabs + A7C menu photos.
- *This is the most locally useful tip Frank has; it should be his pinned post.*
