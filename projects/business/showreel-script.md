# Showreel Script · Frank Willson Talukder · 60–90 s

Owner: John (script) · Cut by: Bartholomew · Music: James-the-less · Check: Thomas · Status: **DRAFT. Nothing is posted without Frank's approval.**

**The one idea:** one quiet filmmaker catches the small, real moments that big crews scare away, at a wedding, at a café table or on a product, all in Dhaka.

Built for the sales plan's three niches, in ranked order: (1) intimate weddings, holud and engagements, (2) restaurants and cafés, (3) small online shops. The reel sells the solo style as a strength: discreet, natural light, close.

## Ground rules for both versions

- **Couple's written OK.** Version A is used only if the Banns couple agrees in writing (WhatsApp or Messenger text counts; keep a screenshot). If they say yes to some shots and not others (for example, no faces, or no voices), cut A to match, or use B. No answer = B.
- **Guests.** Even with the couple's OK, prefer the couple, details and wide shots. No recognisable guest close-ups unless that guest agrees.
- **Music: licensed only.** One track, about 85 s, from James-the-less's licensed library, with the licence covering social and YouTube use. Save the licence or download receipt in the project folder. **Never** use the church's hymns, the restaurant's background music or any track ripped from YouTube or a phone recording.
- **Camera base (matches Friday):** PAL, 4K 25p at 1/50; slow motion 1080p 100p at 1/100 (4x slow at 25p); HLG3 / Pro colour; manual white balance. 25p and 1/50 keep Dhaka's 50 Hz lights from flickering.
- **No drone.** No DJI Neo 2 shots unless Simon confirms the flight was legal and it was actually flown. Both versions work without it.
- **No gear we don't own.** No tripod, gimbal, lights or ND: brace on walls, tables, a stack of books. Window light only indoors.
- **Text on screen** in English, with an optional Bangla line under the end-card headline. Thomas checks spelling in both. The reel must work with the sound off (Facebook autoplays muted).
- **End card CTA:** WhatsApp and Messenger, with placeholders only. Frank fills in his real details in the edit; no number is written here.

### Version A · 9:16 cut-down (Reels / TikTok / Facebook Reels, 30–40 s)

- **Order:** shot 1 hook (3 s), 4, 5, 7, 10, 12 (weddings, about 15 s), 13, 16 (cafés, 5 s), 17 (product, 3 s), 20 (BTS, 3 s), end card (4 s). About 33–36 s.
- **Framing:** 4K 16:9 crops to a 9:16 strip of about 1215 x 2160 px, which is enough for 1080 x 1920. Keep the subject near the centre. **The 1080p 100p slow-mo only gives about 608 x 1080 px when cropped**, which looks soft. Use 100p shots only where the subject is centred and close, or use the 25p version of the moment.
- **Text:** bigger, 3–5 words per card, kept in the middle band. Leave the bottom 20% and the right edge clear for the Reels caption and buttons.
- **Hook:** the first frame must already be the ring. No logo or black frames before it.
- **End card:** "WhatsApp / Messenger · link in bio" plus the same placeholders, held 3 s.
- **Music:** same licensed track, cut to an earlier ending. Check that the licence covers Reels and TikTok. If not, use a track that does.

## Version B shoot plan (one person, after Friday)

Frank is busy 22–23 Oct (Banns prep and shoot), so these go on the free days after: **Sat 24 and Sun 25 Oct** (default; move if needed). Same camera base as above. Both sessions are about 2 hours.

**Session 1 · Home, morning window light (about 2 h)**
- Product shots 9–12: up to 3 items Frank owns, or a friend's online-shop product **with the owner's OK** (a good free sample that could become a lead). Cloth or plain board, the window to the side, a white card or paper as fill.
- Wedding-style details 13–15: borrow a garland, bangles and a ring box; hands from a family member who agrees.
- Gear flat-lay and BTS 16–18 on the phone: set the phone to 25 fps, airplane mode, propped on a stack of books. These can look a little rougher; that's fine for BTS.

**Session 2 · Café, then Dhaka at golden and blue hour (about 2 h, about 16:00–17:50)**
- Café shots 4–8 at about 16:00: ask the owner or manager first, and offer them the clips for their own page (a sample that doubles as outreach). Green Garden Restaurant counts only if the manager agrees on Friday. Otherwise use a café Frank already knows in Dhanmondi.
- Tea stall hook (1) and lake rickshaws (2–3) at golden hour, about 16:45–17:25 (Philip confirms the exact times).
- Blue hour shot (19), about 17:25–17:50.
- Strangers: wide or silhouette only. If someone is clearly recognisable and featured, ask or don't use it. Tea stall: buy a cha and ask the seller first.

**Coverage rule:** hold every shot 8–10 s and get the 100p version of anything that moves. Each shot in the table is 3–4 s on screen, so one good take is enough.
