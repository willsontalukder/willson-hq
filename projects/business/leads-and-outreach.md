## How these leads were checked (read this first)

- Every business below came up by name in a web search (news features, reviews, directory listings), and the link in **Source** is the page where it showed up. No business or link is invented.
- **The source shows the business exists. It is not always the business's own page.** Most results were press coverage; I could not open Facebook or Instagram pages from here. **Before messaging, Frank opens their current Facebook/Instagram page, checks they are still open, and finds one real, recent thing to mention.**
- Some coverage is a few years old (dates noted). A business may have closed or moved, so that 2-minute check matters.
- Only business-level public info is listed: no personal numbers, no private individuals.
- **Prices:** none are given here. Matthew sets packages A–C in BDT; use his numbers in replies.

**Verified: 27 leads** (8 wedding/event, 11 restaurant/café, 8 online shop/brand). The plan's target is 60, so "How to find more" is at the end.

Fit key: **A** = good fit for a solo shooter now. **R** = better as a *referral partner* (they plan or host events; ask to be on their recommended-videographer list, don't pitch them a one-off job).

## 2. Outreach messages (one-to-one, Frank sends himself)

**Rules:** one person at a time; always fill the [brackets] with something real from *their* page; never copy-paste the same text to many; no attachments unless asked; send from Frank's own Facebook page or WhatsApp; max 10 a week. If they say no, thank them and stop.

### Template 1: Wedding planner / decorator / venue

**Banglish**
> Assalamu alaikum [Business name] team! Ami Frank, Dhaka-r ekjon filmmaker. Apnader [specific setup, e.g. "last week-er holud stage-ta"] dekhlam, khub shundor hoyeche.
> Ekta idea: oi decor-er ekta 30–60 sec walk-through video (entrance theke stage porjonto) apnader page-e pin kore rakhle, NRB ba dure thaka family-ra ashol feel ta bujhte parbe.
> Ami chhoto/intimate holud, engagement solo cover kori. Apnader jodi kokhono emon client thake, apnar recommended list-e thakte parle khushi hobo. Interest thakle janaben, kono chap nai. Dhonnobad!

**English**
> Hello [Business name] team! I'm Frank, a filmmaker in Dhaka. I saw your [specific setup] and it looked beautiful.
> One idea: a 30–60 s walk-through of that decor, from the entrance to the stage, pinned on your page, would let families abroad feel the space before they book.
> I film small, intimate holuds and engagements on my own. If you ever have clients like that, I'd be glad to be on your recommended list. No pressure at all; just let me know if it's of interest. Thank you!

### Template 2: Restaurant / café

**Banglish**
> Hi [Business name]! Ami Frank, Dhaka-r ekjon filmmaker. Apnader [dish/space, e.g. "Naanza" / "rooftop-er evening light"] dekhe mone holo eta camera-te darun ashbe.
> Free idea: [dish]-er ekta 15 sec slow-motion close-up (golden hour-e), sathe ekta chhoto caption — Reel hishebe bhalo cholte pare.
> Ami ~2 ghontar ekta shoot-e 3-ta short Reel banai. Chaile ekta chhoto sample bananor kotha bolte pari. Na hole kono shomossha nai, apnader khabar-er jonno shubhechha!

**English**
> Hi [Business name]! I'm Frank, a Dhaka filmmaker. Your [dish/space] looks like it would be great on camera.
> A free idea: a 15 s slow-motion close-up of [dish] at golden hour with a short caption; that kind of Reel tends to do well.
> I make 3 short Reels from one ~2-hour shoot. Happy to talk about a small sample if you're interested. If not, no problem at all, and all the best!

### Template 3: Online shop / small brand

**Banglish**
> Assalamu alaikum [Brand name]! Ami Frank, ekjon filmmaker. Apnader [product, e.g. "Amar Jaan candle"] er concept-ta khub bhalo laglo.
> Ekta idea: janalar alo-te [product]-er ekta 20–30 sec video, [specific moment, e.g. "candle jalano / soap kata / kapor-er drape"] dekhiye — customer-ra photo-te ja bujhe na, video-te sheta bujhe.
> Ami 3-ta product theke 1-ta main video ar 3-ta short cut banai, apnader page/ads-er jonno. Interest thakle janaben, kono tara nai. Dhonnobad!

**English**
> Hello [Brand name]! I'm Frank, a filmmaker. I really liked the idea behind your [product].
> One idea: a 20–30 s window-light video showing [specific moment, e.g. the candle being lit / the soap being cut / the fabric draping]; video shows what a photo can't.
> I make one hero video plus 3 short cuts from up to 3 products, ready for your page or ads. If it interests you, just let me know; no rush. Thank you!

*(Pure-Bangla version on request: Frank can switch any template to Bangla script in his own words. Many owners reply better to the language they post in, so match their page.)*

### Follow-up (once only, 4–5 days later, only if no reply)

**Banglish**
> Hi [Name/Business], ager message-ta hoyto miss hoye geche, tai ekbar mone korie dilam. [Free idea in one line] — ei idea-ta apnara nijerao phone diye try korte paren! Jodi kokhono help lage, janaben. Dhonnobad, bhalo thakben.

**English**
> Hi [Business], just a gentle nudge in case my earlier message got buried. The [one-line idea] is something you could even try on your phone. If you'd ever like a hand with it, I'm here. Thanks, and take care!

After this, **stop**. No second follow-up. Log it in the tracker as "no reply".

## How to find more leads (to reach the plan's 60)

- **Google Maps:** search "café Dhanmondi", "restaurant Banani Road 11", "Gulshan 2 cafe", "community center Dhanmondi", "holud decoration Dhaka". Pick places with good ratings but few or weak videos on their page; note one specific dish/space.
- **Instagram hashtags:** #dhakafoodie #dhakacafe #dhakaeats #gayeholud #bangladeshiwedding #dhakabride #bdonlineshop #handmadebd #madeinbangladesh. Look for brands with good products but phone-shot videos.
- **Facebook groups:** food-lover groups (e.g. Dhaka food review groups), wedding-planning and bride groups, women entrepreneur / small-business groups. Read rules; help in comments; only post promo where allowed; never mass-DM members.
- **Press "new opening" pages:** Daily Star Life & Living, TBS Food, Dhaka Tribune Avenue T, Bangladesh Monitor. New openings need content most.
- **Directories:** Sortlist and Wezoree for Dhaka planners; Business Inspection BD lists (use only as name leads, then verify).
- Add 5–10 a week; each must have a name, area, source link and one real reason before it goes on the tracker.
