# Satkhira school documentary · background score prompts (Suno)

Story: a village in Satkhira that had no school; a donor built one; now the children are learning.
Sound goal: the quiet, pastoral, raga-based feel of 1950s Bengali art cinema (sparse sitar, bansuri, tanpura drone, no drums, lots of silence).

## Before you start

- **Suno blocks artist and film names** in prompts, so the prompts describe the sound instead of naming the film or composer.
- In Suno: **Custom mode ON, Instrumental ON.** Paste "Style" into *Style of Music*, and "Structure" into *Lyrics*.
- Generate 2–4 versions of each cue and keep the most restrained one. Less is more in this style.
- **Licence:** Suno's free plan is for non-commercial use only. If this film goes to a client, a donor, YouTube with ads, or festivals, make the cues on a paid plan, and check Suno's current terms before you publish.
- Mix the score low (about -20 to -24 LUFS under dialogue). Let village sound (birds, water, kids reciting) lead.

## Tips for this sound

- Pick the takes with the **most silence**. If Suno adds drums, pads or a big build, regenerate and add "no drums, no pads, minimal" to the style.
- Use a cue under images with no voice-over, then drop to just the drone (or silence) when someone speaks.
- Record real village sound in Satkhira: birds at dawn, water, a bell, children reciting. Mixed with the score, it feels far more real than music alone.
